# Rescue Boats

The club has three rescue boats:

- **Orange dinghy:** Yamaha 9.9 hp.
  The engine is new in 2026, and the dinghy has its own trailer.
- **Grey dinghy:** Yamaha 25 hp.
  The dinghy has its own trailer.
- **Pioner Multi:** Yamaha 60 hp.
  The engine is new in 2026, and the boat has its own trailer.

The grey dinghy is usually kept on its trailer, ready for callouts in Aurland or during Veko.
The orange dinghy and Pioner Multi are on Lake Vangsvatnet and on standby.

The orange dinghy and Pioner Multi are never locked, so they are available for emergencies.
Always start the engine in neutral and attach the kill-switch lanyard before use.
Follow the [emergency procedures](/en/safety/emergency-procedures), and report faults, damage or maintenance needs to the managing director.

## Mooring

The mooring arrangement on Lake Vangsvatnet is knot-free.
Unclip the carabiner and pull the boat ashore.
To moor it again, pull it back to its berth and clip the carabiner on.

## Rescue Boat Checklist

### 1. Before Launch / Preparing the Boat

#### Engine & Systems

- [ ] Check fuel level.
- [ ] Open the fuel tank air vent valve.
- [ ] Inspect the propeller.
- [ ] Test electric tilt/trim operation.
- [ ] Ensure the emergency stop lanyard is present.
- [ ] Turn the main electrical switch on.
- [ ] Start the engine and verify cooling water discharge.
- [ ] Check VHF radio operation.
- [ ] Verify bow ramp operation (Pioner Multi).

#### Safety & Rescue Equipment

- [ ] Ensure sufficient life jackets/PFDs are onboard.
- [ ] Confirm paddle/oars are onboard.
- [ ] Secure the stretcher/rescue board onboard.
- [ ] Confirm the hook knife is onboard.

### 2. Rescue Operations

- [ ] Open the bow ramp locking hinges before lowering the ramp (Pioner Multi).
- [ ] Approach the casualty slowly and safely.
- [ ] Keep the propeller clear of people in the water.
- [ ] Assign one crew member to maintain visual contact with the casualty.
- [ ] Recover the casualty and secure them onboard.
- [ ] Raise the bow ramp after recovery; the locking hinges do not need to be secured (Pioner Multi).

### 3. After Use

- [ ] Raise the engine using electric tilt.
- [ ] Inspect the propeller for damage.
- [ ] Refill the fuel tank.

### 4. Long-Term Storage

If the boat will remain unused for an extended period:

- [ ] Turn the main electrical switch off.
- [ ] Close the fuel tank air vent valve.
