# Club Car and Transport

The club car takes pilots from landing areas to launch sites around Voss and nearby areas.
Arrange rides in the “Paragliding i Voss og omegn” Facebook group or the “PG/SPG Flyging i Voss og omegn” Messenger community.

## Rules for use

- The driver should enable [Volandoo live tracking](https://volandoo.com/clubs/voss-hpk/live/all) so members can see the car's location.
- The driver is responsible for ensuring everyone pays.
  Rides must not be left unpaid; pay for one another if needed.
- The driver does not pay for the ride they drive if there are more than two people in the car.
- Return the car clean and with a full tank.
- Report faults, damage or maintenance needs to the operations contact.
