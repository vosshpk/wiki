# Clubhouse

The clubhouse is beside Voss Skydiving Club at Flyplassvegen 135.
It is used for courses throughout the year, and the club stores its equipment there.

- All members have access.
  The door code changes regularly; contact the operations contact for the current code.
- Take care of the premises, clean up after use and return equipment to its place.
- Close and lock doors and windows when leaving.

See the [official club facilities page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/fasilitetar-og-skulebakke/){target=_blank} for current information.
