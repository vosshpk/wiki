# Responsibilities, communications and authority

> **To confirm:** This is a role description from a source document, not confirmation of current authority.
> Check it against the bylaws, organisation plan, safety system, and instructions for the board, managing director and head of training before relying on it.

## Board

**Responsibilities:** Overall legal, financial and organisational responsibility.
The board should ensure the club follows applicable requirements and internal policies, approve budgets, strategy and major measures, and support cooperation between the managing director, head of training and safety group.

**Communications:** Receive reports from the managing director on operations and finances, and from the head of training on safety and training.
Maintain dialogue with the safety group through the head of training.

**Authority described in the source:** Make final decisions on major financial and strategic measures.
The source says the board may override professional decisions where legal or financial concerns arise.
Check this wording against current rules and the safety system before relying on it.

## Managing director

**Responsibilities:** Day-to-day operations, including administration, finances and member contact.
Ensure safety and training work has the necessary resources, and coordinate between the board, head of training and safety group.

**Communications:** Report to the board on finances, membership trends and practical challenges.
Work with the head of training to integrate safety into daily operations and inform the safety group about resources and operating limits.

**Authority described in the source:** Make operational and resource decisions within approved financial limits, and initiate safety measures in consultation with the head of training.
See also the [delegation of authority matrix](/en/managing-director/delegation-matrix).

## Head of training

**Responsibilities:** Operational leadership of safety and training, and implementation and maintenance of the club's safety system and rules.
Follow up instructors, tandem pilots and courses in line with current club and NLF requirements.

**Communications:** Work with the managing director on resources and training and safety needs.
Update the board on safety and training, and meet with the safety group to review risks and operational measures.

**Authority described in the source:** Stop activities or courses when risk is unacceptable, make professional decisions within the role, and ensure instructors and members follow safety standards.
Confirm these limits against the current safety system.

## Safety group

**Responsibilities:** Support the head of training in developing and carrying out safety procedures, monitor activities, propose ways to reduce risk and evaluate incidents.

**Communications:** Report deviations and incidents to the head of training, work with the managing director on resources that affect safety, and advise the board on strategic matters.

**Authority described in the source:** Work with the head of training to stop activities considered dangerous and advise the board and managing director.
Confirm this against the safety system.

## Cooperation and decisions

- The board has overall responsibility and sets strategic direction.
- The managing director follows up day-to-day operations and resources.
- The head of training leads operational safety and training work.
- The safety group supports the head of training and advises the board and managing director.

For a safety incident, the source document describes the head of training and safety group as able to stop activities, the managing director as handling practical needs and resources, and the board as considering longer-term measures.
See [communications during incidents](/en/communications/communications-during-incidents) and the [emergency procedures](/en/flying/emergency-procedures).
