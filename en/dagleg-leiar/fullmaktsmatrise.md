# Managing Director Delegation Matrix

> **Status: draft — requires board approval.** Transcribed from a submitted document with no adoption date. Amounts and unclear items are not confirmed as current authority. Until adopted, follow current decisions, budget, bylaws and signing rules.

| Area | Proposed managing director authority | Board approval / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Expenses | Approve budgeted expenses up to **NOK 50,000** | Board approval above NOK 50,000; must be within the board-approved budget. |
| Supplier agreements | Enter agreements up to **NOK 100,000** | Board approval above NOK 100,000; agreements must follow bylaws and applicable rules. |
| Payroll | Pay salaries within budget and board-approved employment agreements | Changes to pay structure require board consideration. |
| New members | Approve applications where membership requirements are met | No separate board approval stated. |
| Excluding members | No authority | Board decides under applicable rules. |
| Events | Plan events within the adopted budget | No separate approval stated within budget. |
| External funding | Apply for sponsorship and grants | Applications must align with the board's strategic goals. |
| Temporary employment | Implement temporary hiring | The source also says “board decides”; authority, staffing plan and budget need clarification. |
| Termination and employment changes | No authority stated | Board decides; follow employment agreements and applicable law. |
| External representation | Represent the club within defined responsibilities | Clarify authority for binding agreements and public statements. |
| Cooperation | Source mentions “SG, Veko, FHS and clubs” | Authority and responsibilities are not completed; board must clarify. |
| Bylaws and strategy | No authority | Changes must be considered by the correct body under the bylaws. |

## Conditions and review

- Authority must remain within the budget approved by the proper body.
- The matrix should fit the club's size, finances and complexity. The board must approve it, record the decision in the minutes, and communicate the limits to the managing director and other relevant people.
- The board should receive regular financial and activity reports.
- Decisions concerning flying, equipment and courses must follow current NLF/NIF rules; consult the safety group's technical assessment where relevant.
- The board should review this matrix at least annually and when the organisation, budget or responsibilities change.
- Clarify how limits apply to multi-year contracts and whether amounts include VAT.
