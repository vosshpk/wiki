# Managing Director Job Description

> **Status:** Transcribed from “Stillingsinstruks DL 2024”. No approval date is provided. This is a working document for board confirmation, not proof of current employment terms.

**Title:** Managing Director  
**Reports to:** Board of Voss Hang- og Paragliderklubb  
**Purpose:** Ensure responsible operations and development in line with the bylaws, NIF rules and club strategy, supporting members through sound administration and financial management.

## Main duties

### Administration

- Help ensure the club follows its bylaws, NIF rules and other relevant law.
- Prepare board matters and follow up decisions from the board and AGM.
- Keep membership records current and follow up joining, leaving and fees.
- Manage the website, social media and information flow, and respond to enquiries.

### Financial management

- Manage funds within the approved budget and delegation matrix, working with the treasurer and any accountant.
- Prepare budget proposals and report regularly on the financial position.
- Prepare applications for public and private funding and follow up sponsors.
- Follow up membership fees and other income.

### Equipment, activities and projects

- Ensure club equipment is maintained, insured and kept in safe condition.
- Make purchases within budget and delegated authority.
- Ensure rules for loans, rentals and use of equipment are followed.
- Maintain an activity calendar and coordinate volunteer work with the board and volunteers.
- Ensure activities and projects follow applicable safety rules and adopted plans.

### Staff, volunteers and cooperation

- Support any employees, including work allocation, training and feedback.
- Coordinate volunteers and clarify tasks and training.
- Maintain cooperation with NLF, local businesses, public bodies and other partners.
- Represent the club at meetings and networks as authorised.

## Authority

The managing director may make decisions in daily operations and make purchases or agreements within the approved budget, board policies and adopted delegation matrix. They may supervise employees and volunteers within delegated responsibility.

## Reporting and cooperation

The managing director regularly reports on finances, operations and activities to the board, works with the board and safety group, and prepares an annual report for the AGM.

## Qualifications

The source mentions administrative and financial experience, knowledge of NIF and Norwegian sports organisations, digital skills, event experience, communication and collaboration skills, organisation, independence and interest in developing the club.

## Working hours and terms

Hours are described as flexible, including evenings and weekends when needed. Pay and employment conditions are set by the board and employment agreement. The employment agreement and applicable law take precedence where they regulate a matter in more detail.
