# Flying Sites around Voss

Voss HPK maintains launch and landing sites for different wind directions, experience levels and seasons.

> This local guide supplements, and does not replace, your own assessment.
Check current forecasts, local wind stations, NOTAMs, airspace at [luftrom.info](https://luftrom.info){target=_blank} and conditions on site.
Fly only with the required licence, insurance and competence. {.is-warning}

## Hanguren and Hangurstoppen

Voss Gondola runs from the town centre to the mountain and provides year-round access.
The club uses two main launches at Hangurstoppen.

### Hangurstoppen East (820 m ASL)

The eastern launch is the main launch for paragliding and speedgliding in summer and winter.

- [Map of the eastern launch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/isYngtzSM43WBmYv6){target=_blank}.
  Take the gondola from Voss town centre to the upper station.
  In winter, the four-seat express lift from Bavallen is another option.
  Walk 200–300 m east from the gondola station.
- Vertical drop to Lake Vangsvatnet (50 m ASL) is about 770 m.
- The grassy slope was improved in 2019/2020 and can be used with or without skis.
- Suitable for a range of levels in calm conditions.
  Crosswind, strong wind and strong thermals require more experience.
  Experienced PP4+ pilots may be able to top-land in winter.
- Light to moderate easterly wind is preferred.
  Gentle morning lift may develop near the launch; thermals often begin around 11:00–12:00.
  Wind often turns southerly later in the day, leaving the eastern launch sheltered or crosswind.
- The stated ceiling is 2,286 m ASL (FL75).
  Check [luftrom.info](https://luftrom.info){target=_blank} and keep clear of Bømoen airfield.

### Hangurstoppen South (about 660 m ASL)

The south launch can be useful for thermal and cross-country flying in southerly winds in spring and summer.

- [Map of the south launch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Fj6ytebL6V3v2AWW9){target=_blank}.
  Take the gondola to the top, then walk about 200 vertical metres down the south side towards Vangen.
- South to south-west facing grassy slope leading to a defined edge with small trees below.
- The source lists this site for paraglider pilots from PP2.
  Thermic conditions require good wing control during launch and departure.
- **Not suitable for speedgliders (SPG).**
- Thermic activity is common from March/April to September, often from 13:00–14:00; typical cloud base is stated as 1,500–2,000 m.
- Southerly wind up Bordalen may be strong and turbulent.
  A westerly sea breeze often develops over the lake in the afternoon from May/June and may make the launch unusable.
  Rokne or Liaset may be alternatives in westerlies.
- Gondola cables pass just east of the launch.
  Keep well clear, especially when a westerly component could drift you towards the cables.

## Training Hill at Bakkatun

The training hill by Bømoen is the club's regular practice area for students and pilots.
It is used for ground handling, basic training and low training hops.

- **Location:** Bømoen/Bakkatun, by Voss Skydiving Club.
- **Purpose:** Courses and independent practice outside course sessions.
- **Season:** Spring, summer and autumn; take particular care during the growing season.
- The hill may be used freely when grass is short or freshly cut.
  Activity is restricted when grass is high before mowing.
  If unsure whether the hill is open, contact the club manager at [malene@vosshpk.no](mailto:malene@vosshpk.no).
- The main hill, marked A, is the regular practice area.
  Areas B, C and D require prior agreement with the landowner, for example during a course.
- Do not park in farmyards or drive/park on the gravel road.
  Use the designated parking by the skydiving club and walk to the hill.
- Take away rubbish, tape, plastic and lines.
  Leave the site clean.
- The club stores training equipment and holds theory sessions in the [clubhouse at Bømoen](/en/club-equipment/clubhouse-at-bomoen).

## Landing Areas on Voss

1. **Prestegardslandet/Kulturhuset (50 m ASL):** Main landing by Lake Vangsvatnet.
   Strong easterlies, especially with a northerly component, can cause rotor.
   Helicopters, including the air ambulance, use the western side.
   See [local flying safety rules](/en/safety/local-flying-safety-rules).
2. **Minigolfen:** Grass landing by the promenade, best in calm conditions and light southerly winds.
3. **Ringheim:** Large field below the eastern launch.
   Closed during the summer growing season; may be used when crops are not growing.
4. **Grandane:** Gravel bank at the mouth of the Vosso, usable when water levels are low.

**Not approved for landing:** Mølstertunet and the bottom of the Bavallen ski jump.

### Høgåsen, Aurland

For flying in Aurland, the club lists a landing fee of **NOK 10 per flight**, up to **NOK 50 per day**.

Pay by Vipps to “Voss HPK Flyavgift Aurland” or to account **9581.05.18384**, using “landingsavgift Aurland” as the reference.
