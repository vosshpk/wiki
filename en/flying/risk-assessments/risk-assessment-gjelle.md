## Risk assessment

| No. | Event/situation | Cause | Risk/consequence | Mitigation | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Aborted launch - pilot fails to take off. | The “point of no return” has not been set, may be poorly placed or may not be followed. Poor choice of the run-up line for wind direction and terrain. Technique, not wing control. Wing glide ratio is not suitable for the steepness of the launch. | Minor injury, such as a twisted ankle or scraped wrist. If the aborted launch happens late in the run, there is a risk of rolling farther down the terrain. This may cause impact and/or fall injury. | Focus on routine and active use of the point of no return to abort in time. Wing control and good knowledge of one's own equipment. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 2 | Sink or collapse on departure flight | Lee side in west wind, rotor wind from the south or sink area in thermic conditions. Cold-air drainage. Low speed at takeoff, and thus the wing is not fully pressurised. | Emergency landing or crash landing in a tree or terrain. | Knowledge of and consideration for local conditions, clearing forest below launch, good wing control. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 3 | Takeoff in unsuitable conditions | Reluctance to go down again. Stress at launch; spectators, sheep, group dynamics, cameras and fitness. Or a change in weather conditions that has not been registered. | Critical situations in the air, and potentially demanding landing conditions. | Focus on building attitudes, knowledge of risk assessment, meteorology and understanding of local conditions. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 4 | Tree landing | Ridge soaring close to terrain, e.g. at a marginal ridge (thermic, but especially mechanical). Flying in conditions that are too advanced for one's competence level. | Injury to pilot and wing | Focus on not overestimating one's own skills and the conditions | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 5 | Collapse over land and reserve deployment | Incorrect execution of exercises at low altitude, e.g. during training. | Emergency landing/crash landing. There is a risk from high-voltage lines at the landing site, river and water. | Good margin in the flight plan adapted to competence level | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 6 | Airspace conflict | For example, skydiving aircraft or glider tow often have flight paths near Gjelle. | Uncomfortable experience of flying close to one another; in the worst case, a collision if two pilots are inattentive and neither uses other visibility tools. | Use SafeSky or similar and inform others about local airspace activity. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 7 | Misjudging actual wind strength | Terrain formations and weather-station location | Takeoff in rotor | Check nearby weather stations to get a good picture of actual mean wind strength and direction. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
