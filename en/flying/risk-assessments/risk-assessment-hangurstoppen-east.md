## Risk assessment

| No. | Event/situation | Cause | Risk/consequence | Mitigation | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Aborted launch: slip/trip, front collapse or asymmetric collapse | Inadequate flight plan, “point of no return”, line/distance assessment, weather or personal skill; crosswind or light wind. | Fall and muscle/joint injury, typically cuts, impact injury or sprain. | Progression course focused on launch technique and wing control through ground handling. | 2 | 1 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 2 | Tree snag/landing | Poor flight plan or assessment of weather/skills during manoeuvre. | Fall; muscle/joint injury, cuts, impact, back or suspension injury. | Remove trees and bushes. | 1 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 3 | Launch with a twist | Ground handling in a twist lock; wind increase can lift the pilot the wrong way, or a reverse launch may turn the wrong way. | Uncontrolled takeoff and unwanted turn before clearing terrain, leading to crash landing. | Practise wing control and ground handling; practise bringing the wing down effectively. | 1 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 4 | Canopy collision (ridge/thermal and PG/SPG approach) | Poor communication at launch and omitted five-point check. | Serious injury to two people. | Focus on flight planning and right-of-way rules. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 5 | Gondola cable and other lines | Inadequate route planning can reduce clearance. Misjudged weather or fixation on another event may cause loss of awareness. | Serious pilot injury and possible third-party property damage. | Establish a safety zone. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 6 | Losing a loose object over buildings | Poor equipment check. | Injury or damage to third parties/property. | Provide information and reminders to secure equipment. | 1 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 7 | Emergency landing | Poor flight plan, major altitude loss during manoeuvres, or inadequate assessment/change in weather. | Secondary injury from a poor landing. | Map alternative landing sites and clarify decision altitude. | 2 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 8 | Collapse in mechanical or thermal turbulence near terrain | Weather and pilot skill. | May lead to a secondary spin and crash landing; potentially high consequence. | Progression training, weather knowledge and risk assessment. | 3 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> |
| 9 | Water landing | Reserve deployment. | Potentially severe consequences in cold conditions or poor ice conditions. | Rescue boat. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 10 | Sink after launch/in flight | Rotor, cold-air drainage or poor launch technique, such as sitting down too early. | Back or ankle injury. | Use a foam-padded PG harness, practise launch technique and improve weather/meteorology assessment skills. | 2 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 11 | Dragging during ground handling at launch | Misjudged conditions, wind strength or pilot skills. | From abrasions to serious injury. | Assess terrain. | 2 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 12 | Airspace conflict with helicopter/aircraft | Lack of airspace awareness and digital visibility. | Risk of harm to third parties. | Use tracking, stay alert and be willing to yield. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 13 | Injury during top landing | Misjudged conditions, wind or skills. | Minor to serious pilot injury, commonly ankle or compression fracture; possible third-party/property damage. | Plan the flight, account for fitness and keep room to abort the approach. | 1 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 14 | Equipment fault: cravat/line-over/tension knot, asymmetric trim, out-of-trim wing or locked/tangled brake line | Fitness, poor inspection routine, lack of focus or flight plan without a clear “point of no return”. | Aborted launch or emergency landing. | Routine equipment checks and knowledge of own equipment. | 2 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 15 | Takeoff in unsuitable conditions | Spectators, high activity, queues and limited space; gondola makes the launch easily accessible, and cameras distract. | Aborted launch/emergency landing; stress may lead to poor flight planning or spontaneous advanced manoeuvres. | Account for group dynamics and human factors. | 2 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> |
