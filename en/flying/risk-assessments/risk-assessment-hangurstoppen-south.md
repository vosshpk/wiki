## Risk assessment

| No. | Event/situation | Cause | Risk/consequence | Mitigation | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Stress at launch | Tourists, spectators and sheep; group dynamics and cameras. | Stress reduces focus and may lead to poor assessment or launch technique. | Help one another, allow for daily condition and give each other time for equipment checks. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 2 | Front collapse/collapse during takeoff | Changing conditions, uneven acceleration or unloading over rough ground, trees, bushes and tall grass; poor line choice can unload the wing. | Landing/crash in terrain immediately after launch, injuring pilot and/or wing. | Smooth vegetation and focus training on terrain and line choice. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 3 | Insufficient takeoff speed | Technique or misjudgement of wind strength/tailwind. | Aborted launch or landing in terrain just after launch, injuring pilot and/or wing. | Maintain margins, know equipment and skills, and encourage ground handling. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 4 | Sink or collapse after launch | Lee-side conditions in east/west crosswind, e.g. sea-breeze or sink in thermic conditions. Common later in the day when flying conditions are good. | Late abort/failed launch may cause minor injury to pilot and/or wing. | Assess conditions and local terrain. Build a culture of avoiding launches in suboptimal wind directions. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 5 | Flying in strong wind or rain | Strong desire to fly and reluctance to descend or climb back up can make it difficult to see the full picture of the forecast and conditions. Convergence lift, wave conditions or fronts may cause rapid changes. | A critical in-flight situation involving collapse or a parachutal stall. Even if the flight goes well, the pilot may feel they are losing control or in danger. | Check weather stations and available information on local weather phenomena. Put up signs at launch. A difference between forecast and recorded wind is a sign of unpredictability. The same applies to a forecast showing different weather types, wind directions and wind strengths through the day. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 6 | Flying in strong thermals, especially in spring or early progression | An easily accessible launch with high activity and many wings in the air can challenge a pilot's willingness to stop flying. Equipment with performance or aspect ratio beyond the pilot's experience may make turbulence and collapse harder to handle. Lack of SIV training or understanding. | In-flight collapse, possibly followed by reserve deployment. | Use equipment suited to the pilot's level with good margins. Build progression for beginning thermal flying and make a flight plan for learning new equipment. Arrange an organised introduction to flying in lift for PP2/PP3 pilots. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 7 | Gondola cable | Loss of control while crossing the cable, e.g. collapse in turbulence or insufficient clearance. Focus on route planning/soaring may hide obstacles and altitude loss. | Collision with cable/cabin and crash landing. | Cross the cable with the greatest possible clearance and height. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 8 | Landing in trees/crash | Collapse in turbulence while soaring close to terrain, especially mechanical lift; also proxy flying on SPG. | Emergency/crash landing with serious injury to pilot and equipment. | Adapt each flight plan to skills and conditions. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 9 | Losing loose objects | Inadequate securing and awareness while focusing on the flight plan. | Damage to houses or third parties. | Regularly remind pilots to secure items, especially skis in winter and cameras/other equipment year-round. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 10 | Canopy collision | No prior shared flight plan; different speeds for SPG, PG and skydiving. Relevant during busy periods/events. | Reserve deployment or crash landing injuring one or two pilots; third-party injury over buildings. | Give extra information during busy activity/events and clearly communicate rules. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 11 | Reserve deployment over water | Manoeuvre practice, acro or SIV. | Injury during emergency landing; drowning risk if rescue boat is not staffed or it is winter. | Have two rescue boats in season and staff a boat during courses. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
