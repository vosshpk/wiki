## Risk assessment

| No. | Event/situation | Cause | Risk/consequence | Mitigation | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Stress at launch | Tourists, spectators, sheep. Group dynamics and cameras. | Misjudging conditions or poor technical execution of launch due to stress leads to less focus. | Culture of helping one another, allow margins for daily condition and give oneself and one another time for a good equipment check. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 2 | Front collapse/collapse in the takeoff phase | Change in conditions, uneven acceleration or unloading on uneven terrain, trees, bushes and tall grass. Pressure relief in the wing often comes from incorrect line choice regarding unloading in the terrain. | Landing/crash in the terrain just after launch and thus injury to pilot and/or wing. | Levelling of vegetation, focus on terrain and line choice in training. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 3 | Low takeoff speed | Technique and/or misjudgement of conditions regarding wind speed or tailwind | Aborted launch/landing in the terrain just after launch and thus injury to pilot and/or wing. | Margin in launch conditions, know one's own equipment and skills. Encourage ground handling. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 4 | Sink or collapse after launch | Lee side in crosswind (east/west), e.g. sea breeze or sink area in thermic conditions. This is a very common problem that occurs later in the day when flying conditions are good. | Late aborted launch/failed launch ending in minor injury to pilot and/or wing. | Focus on assessing conditions and the influence of local terrain formations. Build attitudes against using the launch in a sub.optimal wind direction. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 5 | Flying in strong wind, rain. | There may be a strong desire to fly, and reluctance to go down/up again can mean one does not see the whole picture regarding forecast and conditions. Convergence lift, wave conditions, weather front causing rapid changes. | Critical situation in the air regarding collapse or parachutal stall. If the flight goes well, it can still be an unpleasant experience to lose control or be in danger. | Weather stations, available information about local weather phenomena. Sign at launch. A difference between forecast and recorded wind is a sign of unpredictability. The same is a forecast reporting different weather types, wind directions and wind strength through the day. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 6 | Flying in sharp thermals (especially in spring or early in progression) | An easily accessible launch encourages a lot of activity. Many wings in the air can challenge the desire to fly. Equipment with performance/aspect ratio above one's experience level makes one less suited to handle turbulence and collapse. Lack of SIV training/understanding. | Collapse in the air with possible subsequent reserve deployment | Focus on equipment suited to your level with good margin. Progression for beginning thermal flying as well as a flight plan for learning new equipment. Facilitate an organised introduction to flying in lift for PP2/PP3 pilots. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 7 | Gondola cable | Loss of control while crossing cable - e.g. collapse in turbulence. Or insufficient margin when crossing the cable. Too much focus on flight plan and ridge soaring may mean loss of overview of obstacles and altitude loss. | Collision with cable or cabin with wing/pilot and crash landing. | Clear encouragement to make a flight plan crossing the cable with the greatest possible margin and height. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 8 | Tree landing/crash | Collapse due to turbulence while ridge soaring close to terrain, e.g. at a marginal ridge (thermic, but especially mechanical). Proxy flying SPG | Emergency landing or crash in the terrain with further damage and serious injuries to pilot with equipment. | Focus on a flight plan for each flight adapted to skills and conditions. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 9 | Losing loose objects | Inadequate securing, focus on flight plan and awareness of surroundings | Damage to houses and third party | Constant reminder about this, especially regarding flying with skis in winter. Also relevant for cameras and other things throughout the year. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 10 | Canopy collision | Too little clarification of the flight plan beforehand if planning to fly together. Conflict between gliders with different speeds: SPG/PG/skydiving. A real issue with high activity in the season and events. | Possible reserve deployment or crash landing injuring 1-2 pilots. If over buildings, injury to a third party. | Extra information and reminders during high activity and planned events. Clearly inform about guidelines. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 11 | Reserve deployment over water | Training manoeuvres, acro or SIV. | Injury during emergency landing, risk of drowning if there is no staffed boat and it is winter. | Two rescue boats in season, staffed during courses. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
