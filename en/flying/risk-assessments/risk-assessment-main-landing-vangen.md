# Main landing, Vangen

| Field | Information |
|---|---|
| Club | Voss Hang- og Paragliderklubb |
| Site | Main landing, Vangen (Fleischer/cultural centre) |
| Prepared by | Malene Hetle Hole |
| Date in source | 03.09.2026 |
| Classification in source | Internal |

*Source document: “Sikker utførelse av hang-, para- og speedgliding” (risk analysis).*
**Likelihood scale in source:** 1 low, 2 medium, 3 high.
**Consequence scale in source:** 1 low, 2 medium, 3 high.


## Risk assessment

| No. | Event/situation | Cause | Risk/consequence | Mitigation | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Collision on approach | No approach plan, impulsive changes, unclear landing pattern or heavy traffic. | Too little time to deploy reserve; crash landing may injure two pilots and a third party. | Clear landing-pattern rules. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 2 | Conflict with spectators/passers-by, possibly a drone | People unaware of air activity; insufficient information for third parties. | Collision injury to pilot or third party. | Signage and consider barriers. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 3 | Landing into an object: VOSS sign, stone steps or stone table | Overshoot/undershoot from miscalculation, no approach plan, or poor assessment of landing conditions/wing performance. | Pilot injury, especially if the wing has not flared and the pilot cannot brake effectively. | Train club pilots and encourage extra margin at the main landing. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 4 | Water landing | Towing activity using this landing area, SIV/acro manoeuvres over water, a misjudged approach, increased wind or incorrect positioning, for example in strong east wind. Another possible cause is a planned raft landing. | Moderate to serious injury depending on flare and speed, hypothermia and possible drowning if there is a problem with the boat or it is unstaffed. | Plan this type of activity with a staffed boat. Pilots must prepare suitable equipment, a life jacket and a hook knife. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 5 | Catching a lamp post/tree | Misjudged approach and loss of focus due to distractions while landing. This is especially possible in east wind, particularly when wind from the east is increasing. The cultural centre and Vangen are large airflow obstacles, and increasing wind greatly increases the turbulence and area affected by rotor flows. | Injury to wing and pilot, and possible injury to a third party on the footpath. | Explain all obstacles in briefings and on information signs. Avoid approaches with low turns close to the road. Encourage generous clearance from all approach obstacles and use a pattern over more open areas. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 6 | Collapse on landing due to turbulence | This is especially possible in east wind, particularly when wind from the east is increasing. The cultural centre and Vangen are large airflow obstacles. As wind increases, turbulence and the area affected by rotating airflows increase considerably. | Hard landing and injury resulting from it. | Teach understanding of turbulence and weather conditions. Adapt the approach pattern to maintain good clearance from the cultural centre, water and other obstacles in these conditions. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 7 | Helicopter at landing | An incident at the landing site or another local incident, such as a rescue operation or event. | Distraction, conflict with helicopter operations or turbulence on approach. | Share Voss airspace activity so pilots are prepared for it to happen. Notify communication groups as soon as the information is known. Plan alternative landing sites, for example Ringheim and Prestegardslandet. Monitor the airspace and give helicopters room. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 8 | Ground handling on landing area during activity | Courses or training with many pilots at Voss, for example in summer or during Ekstremsportveko. | Landing collision or evasive manoeuvre leading to a poor landing. | Avoid this during organised activity. Always monitor airspace so pilots who are landing can be given room in good time. Prestegardslandet is the best place for ground handling in west wind. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> |
| 9 | Catching a stone slab | Touchdown just before a slab may cause a trip. | Fall during landing/roll-out. | Remove slabs if the landowner permits. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 10 | Hard landing | Low turn, loss of height awareness, inadequate planning/training or impulsive flight-plan changes. | Pilot injury, potentially serious at high speed. | Landing pattern and AM course. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 11 | Winter water landing | Manoeuvres over water or misjudged approach. | Landing injury, severe hypothermia and drowning when the rescue boat is not on the water. | Explain the winter risk when the boat is not on the water. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 12 | Impulsive fast or advanced landing for filming | Human factors, especially friends, spectators and cameras. | Hard landing, collision with people or miscalculation. | Teach and emphasise human factors. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> |
