## Risk assessment

| No. | Event/situation | Cause | Risk/consequence | Mitigation | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Pilot gets stuck in ski lift (T-bar on the way up) | Poor equipment check and lack of awareness. | Injury to pilot/equipment and lift stoppage. | Check equipment. | 1 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 2 | Takeoff with malfunction: tension knot, cravat or line-over | Gentle terrain and many possible runs may reduce attention to equipment checks; inadequate wing preparation before “point of no return”. | Failure to take off and stopping below takeoff; emergency landing in terrain. | Check equipment. | 2 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 3 | No lift/airspeed before planned takeoff point | Tailwind, low slope angle or wet snow slowing skis. | Stops on steeper terrain below launch. | Assess weather, terrain, snow, wing, size, skis and glide together. | 2 | 1 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 4 | Unable to stop at “point of no return” | Inadequate skiing or stopping skills reduce mental capacity. | Malfunction or crash landing below launch. | Follow up in courses and focus on ski technique. | 1 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 4a | Collision with skiers | Skiers may enter from the side when crossing a piste after the lift, during takeoff, riding or landing. | High-speed collision causing injury to pilot, skier and equipment. | Emphasise the five-point check and airspace awareness during courses and throughout the season. | 2 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> |
| 5 | Pilot in piste/lift corridor | Insufficient margin in flight plan. | High-speed collision injuring pilot, skier and equipment. | Keep at least 20 m clearance in all directions from pistes and lifts. Take off parallel to them. Account for wind/crosswind to avoid drifting over pistes or lifts. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 6 | Crash in creek gully while flying or skiing | Flight plan with little margin, flat light or seasonal terrain changes. | High-speed collision and injury to pilot/equipment. | Flying close to terrain increases risk and demands experience, skill, planning, multi-factor assessment and mental capacity. Build awareness; the same applies to off-piste skiing. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 7 | Slab avalanche release on steep slope | Unstable snow and insufficient understanding/competence. Travel outside the ski area is at one's own risk. | A larger release may carry skiers downhill and endanger people below. | Focus on avalanche terrain, runout zones and forecasts in beginner courses. Do not enter steep terrain when uncertain. Encourage continuous learning and assessment. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 8 | Losing a ski in flight | Missing retention strap. | A ski falling at speed may injure people or damage buildings; it may also cause the pilot to fall. | Retention straps for tech bindings or binding locks are mandatory. Cover safety/rules in courses; avoid flying over people/buildings where possible. | 1 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 9 | Collision with an obstacle on landing | Approach with too little margin, changing wind or target fixation. | High-speed collision, injury to pilot and damage to equipment. | Plan each approach early, assess weather, look ahead and choose a large open area. Practise precision landings throughout progression; account for slope, terrain and obstacles with generous margin. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 10 | Barrel rolls/advanced low-altitude manoeuvres | Myrkdalen does not provide enough height for safe practice with small wings. | High-speed crash and potentially serious injury. | Practise wing control while riding; avoid manoeuvres causing large altitude loss. For more challenge, practise controlled riding on steeper terrain with a smaller turn radius. | 1 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 11 | Pilot crashes on skis travelling to takeoff or home | Poor skiing skills, excessive speed, fatigue or desire to ski fast. | Injury to self or others. | Reflect on fitness and ability. | 2 | 2 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 12 | Wind increase while flying/kiting | Optimal wind in Myrkdalen is south/southeast and kiting wind can create wave conditions. Too little flight-plan margin for forecast/possible changes. | Dragging, rotor, collapse and crash causing pilot injury. | Wave conditions increase risk. Check weather/wind and gust trends. Plan for experience, skill, equipment and terrain. Stronger wind calls for more terrain behind to stop/control the wing before rotor; keep away from the strongest lift zone. | 2 | 3 | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> |
