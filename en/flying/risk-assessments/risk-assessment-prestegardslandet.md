## Risk assessment

| No. | Event/situation | Cause | Risk/consequence | Mitigation | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Collision on approach - more relevant when pilots land at the main landing and Prestegardslandet at the same time. E.g. Ekstremsportveko | Missing approach plan in the flight plan, impulsive change of plan, unclear approach pattern, heavy traffic | Too little time to throw the reserve parachute, resulting in a crash landing with possible injury to two pilots and a third party. | Clear guidelines for the landing pattern and early clarification. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 2 | Conflict with spectators and passers-by at landing, potentially with a drone | Tourists and people are not aware of airspace activity. Lack of information for third parties. | Injury to pilot and third party in a collision | Signage and assess barriers | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 3 | Landing into an object: tree, outdoor stage, fences and similar. | Overshoot or undershoot the landing due to miscalculation. No approach plan in case of miscalculation and possible landing conditions or wing performance. | Injury to pilot, especially if the wing has not flared and the pilot cannot brake well. | Training for club pilots and encourage extra margin at our main landing. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 4 | Water landing | Particularly relevant during towing activity where this landing is used. Other causes as usual are manoeuvres over water during SIV/acro training. Misjudged approach or increased wind speed and being in the wrong position. This can happen, for example, in strong east wind. Another cause may be a planned raft landing. | Moderate to serious injury on landing depending on flare curve and speed. Further hypothermia and possible drowning if there is a problem with the boat or it is unstaffed. | Plan these types of activities with a staffed boat. Pilots make their own preparations regarding suitable equipment, life jacket and hook knife. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 5 | Catching a lamp post/tree | Misjudged approach, loss of focus due to distractions on landing. This is quite possible in east wind, especially with increasing wind from the east. The cultural centre and Vangen are generally a large obstacle in the airflow. With increased wind speed, turbulence and the area affected by these rotating airflows increase considerably. | Injury to wing and pilot, possibly third parties walking on the footpath. | Inform about all obstacles in briefings and information signs. Avoid approaches that require low turns close to the road. Encourage good margin from all obstacles on approach and place the pattern over more open areas. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 6 | Collapse on landing due to turbulence | This is quite possible in east wind, especially with increasing wind speed from the east. The cultural centre and Vangen are generally a large obstacle in the airflow. With increased wind speed, turbulence and the area affected by these rotating airflows increase considerably. | Hard landing and injury in connection with it | Training about understanding turbulence and weather conditions. Adapt the approach pattern to provide good clearance from the cultural centre, and also water and other obstacles in these conditions. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 7 | Helicopter at landing | Incident at landing or another local incident involving rescue or an event | Distraction, landing in conflict with helicopter operation, turbulence on approach. | Inform about airspace activity in Voss so people are prepared this may happen. Inform communication groups as soon as one has information. Encourage having alternative landing sites in the flight plan. E.g. Ringheim and Prestegardslandet. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 8 | Ground handling at landing during activity | Courses or training, a large number of pilots at Voss, e.g. summer or Ekstremsportveko. | Collision on landing or evasive manoeuvres that lead to a poor landing. | During organised activity we avoid this. Always look up at the airspace so one can give space in good time to those who are going to land. The best alternative in west wind is to use Prestegardslandet for ground handling. | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> |
| 9 | (Impulsive) desire to land at higher speed, close to friends or in a cool way. | Human factor: especially spectators, cameras. | Hard landing, collision with people, miscalculation. | Teaching and focus on human factor | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--3">3</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--1">1</span> | <span class="voss-ros-risk-level voss-ros-risk-level--2">2</span> |
| 10 | Hard landing | Low turn, loss of height awareness. May be due to lack of planning and training. Or an impulsive change of flight plan. | Injury to pilot, potentially serious injuries at high speed. | Landing pattern and AM course.
