| No. | Event/situation | Cause | Risk/consequence | Mitigation | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Collision on approach, especially when pilots use both the main landing and Prestegardslandet, e.g. during Ekstremsportveko | No approach plan, impulsive changes, unclear pattern or heavy traffic. | Too little time to deploy reserve; crash landing may injure two pilots and a third party. | Clear landing-pattern rules and early coordination. | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| 2 | Conflict with spectators/passers-by, possibly a drone | People unaware of air activity; insufficient information for third parties. | Collision injury to pilot or third party. | Signage and consider barriers. | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | Landing into a tree, outdoor stage, fence or similar | Overshoot/undershoot from miscalculation, no approach plan, or poor assessment of landing conditions/wing performance. | Pilot injury, especially if the wing has not flared and the pilot cannot brake effectively. | Train club pilots and encourage extra margin at the main landing. | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| 4 | Water landing | Towing activity using this landing area, SIV/acro over water, misjudged approach, increased wind or planned raft landing. | Moderate to serious injury, hypothermia and possible drowning if the boat is absent or unstaffed. | Plan these activities with a staffed boat. Pilots should bring suitable equipment, life jacket and hook knife. | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 5 | Catching a lamp post/tree | Misjudged approach and distractions. In east wind, Vangen/cultural centre affects airflow and increases turbulence. | Injury to wing/pilot or a third party on the footpath. | Brief and signpost obstacles. Avoid low turns near the road, keep clearance and use a more open landing pattern. | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| 6 | Collapse on landing due to turbulence | East wind, especially increasing wind, makes Vangen/cultural centre a significant airflow obstacle. | Hard landing and injury. | Teach turbulence and weather awareness. Adapt the approach to clear the cultural centre, water and other obstacles. | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| 7 | Helicopter at landing | Local incident, rescue operation or event. | Distraction, conflict with helicopter operations or turbulence on approach. | Share airspace activity as soon as known, including communication groups. Plan alternatives such as Ringheim and Prestegardslandet. Monitor airspace and give helicopters room. | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| 8 | Ground handling on landing area during activity | Courses/training with many pilots, e.g. in summer or during Ekstremsportveko. | Landing collision or evasive manoeuvre leading to poor landing. | Avoid during organised activity. Prestegardslandet is better suited for ground handling in west wind. | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| 9 | Impulsive landing at higher speed or advanced landing for filming | Human factors, especially friends, spectators and cameras. | Hard landing, collision with people or miscalculation. | Teach and emphasise human factors. | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| 10 | Hard landing | Low turn, loss of height awareness, inadequate planning/training or impulsive flight-plan changes. | Pilot injury, potentially serious at high speed. | Landing pattern and AM course; apply competence limits during events. | 1 | 3 | 2 |
