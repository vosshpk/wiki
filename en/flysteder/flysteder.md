# Flying sites around Voss

Voss HPK maintains launch and landing sites for different wind directions, experience levels and seasons.

> [!WARNING]
> This local guide supplements, and does not replace, your own assessment. Check current weather, local wind stations, NOTAMs and airspace at [luftrom.info](https://luftrom.info), as well as conditions on site. Fly only with the required licence, insurance and competence.

## Landing areas on Voss

1. **Prestegardslandet / Kulturhuset (50 m):** Main landing by Lake Vangsvatnet. Be aware of helicopter activity on the western side and turbulence in strong easterly winds.
2. **Minigolfen:** Grass landing near the promenade, best in calm conditions and light southerly winds.
3. **Ringheim:** Large field below the eastern launch. **Closed during the summer growing season.**
4. **Grandane:** Gravel bank at the mouth of the Vosso, usable when water levels are low.

### Prohibited landing areas

- **Mølstertunet:** Do not land here.
- **Bavallen ski jump:** The bottom of the ski jump is not an approved landing area.

## Shared transport

The club car provides rides to several launches. See [facilities and transport](/en/klubben/fasiliteter#club-car-and-shared-transport) for rules and current prices.

## Wind and weather information

The club publishes wind readings for Gjelle, Hangur South, Hangur East, the main Kulturhuset landing, Rokne and Ondrahaugen in Myrkdalen. See the [current weather and wind overview](https://vosshpk.no/fly-pa-voss/vaer-og-vind/). Readings are only one input; assess conditions on site as well.
