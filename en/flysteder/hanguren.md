# Hanguren and Hangurstoppen

Hanguren and Hangurstoppen are central flying sites on Voss. Voss Gondola runs from the town centre to the mountain, providing year-round access for paraglider and speedglider pilots. The club uses two main launches: **Hangurstoppen East** and **Hangurstoppen South**.

## Hangurstoppen East (820 m ASL)

The eastern launch is the main launch for paragliding and speedgliding in summer and winter.

- **Access:** [Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/isYngtzSM43WBmYv6). Take the gondola from Voss town centre to the upper station. In winter, the four-seat express lift from Bavallen is another option. Walk 200–300 m east from the gondola station.
- **Vertical drop:** About 770 m to Lake Vangsvatnet (50 m ASL).
- **Direction:** East to north-east.
- **Terrain:** Grassy slope improved in 2019/2020. Suitable with or without skis.
- **Experience:** Suitable for a range of levels in calm conditions. Light, cross, strong or strongly thermic winds require more experience. Experienced PP4+ pilots may be able to top-land in winter.
- **Typical conditions:** Light to moderate easterly wind is preferred. Gentle morning lift may develop near the launch; thermals often begin around 11:00–12:00. Wind often turns southerly later in the day, leaving the eastern launch sheltered or crosswind; the south launch may be an option.
- **Airspace:** The stated ceiling is 2,286 m ASL (FL75). Check [luftrom.info](https://luftrom.info) and keep clear of Bømoen airfield.

## Hangurstoppen South (about 660 m ASL)

The south launch can be useful for thermal and cross-country flying in southerly winds in spring and summer.

- **Access:** [Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Fj6ytebL6V3v2AWW9). Take the gondola, then walk about 200 vertical metres down the south side towards Vangen.
- **Direction:** South to south-west.
- **Terrain:** Grassy slope leading to a defined edge with small trees below.
- **Experience:** The club page lists this launch for paraglider pilots from PP2. Thermic conditions require good wing control during launch and departure.
- **Not suitable for speedgliders (SPG).**
- **Typical conditions:** Thermic activity from around March/April to September, often from 13:00–14:00; typical cloud base is stated as 1,500–2,000 m. Southerly wind up Bordalen may be strong and turbulent. A westerly sea breeze often develops over the lake in the afternoon from May/June and may make the launch unusable. Rokne or Liaset may be alternatives in westerlies.
- **Cable hazard:** Gondola cables pass just east of the launch. Keep well clear, especially when a westerly component could drift you towards the cables after take-off.

## Landing areas

Landing options depend on wind and season. Assess conditions on site.

### Prestegardslandet / Kulturhuset (50 m ASL)

[Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/K9bUAvyuhVTpoKWw7), by the lower gondola station and Lake Vangsvatnet. Often suitable in calm conditions and westerlies. Strong easterlies, especially with a northerly component, can cause rotor and turbulence; Ringheim may be an option outside the growing season. Helicopters, including the air ambulance, regularly use the western side. See [local flying safety rules](/en/sikkerhet/flytrygging).

### Minigolfen

May work in calm conditions and light southerly winds. Easterlies can cause turbulence.

### Ringheim

[Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/oRdgcwCtGkXYZB2x7). Large cultivated field below the eastern launch, with a windsock. Landings are usually towards the north. Closed during the summer growing season; may be used when the field is not growing crops.

### Grandane

Gravel bank at the mouth of the Vosso, usable as a landing area when water levels are low.

### Not approved for landing

Do not land at **Mølstertunet**, a protected museum and cultivated land, or at the **bottom of the Bavallen ski jump**.
