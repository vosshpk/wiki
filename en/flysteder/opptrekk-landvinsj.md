# Winch towing procedures

This is the English version of Voss HPK's procedure for the DaWinchi ground winch, dated 12 September 2026. It says NLF/HPS approval is required before towing activity begins. Confirm approval, revision status and local conditions with the club before use. This page is not an independent approval.

Read the [Nynorsk Markdown transcription](/flysteder/opptrekk-landvinsj) for the procedure, roles, checks and local instructions. The source document covers:

- site approval, NOTAMs, landowner permission and keeping the tow area clear;
- pilot, operator, instructor and ground-crew qualifications;
- planning, equipment checks, setup and winch connection;
- ground-operator procedures and emergency responses;
- self-winch operation; and
- pilot procedures before, during and after tow, including line breaks and release.

The source PDF includes equipment photos. The Markdown page displays extracted copies from the repository archive.
