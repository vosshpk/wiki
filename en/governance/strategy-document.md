## Introduction and purpose

The board of Voss Hang- and Paraglider Club wants a comprehensive, sustainable and long-term effort to facilitate stable and good conditions for hang gliding, paragliding and speed gliding in Voss.
This document is a contribution to securing development.
The draft strategy document 2024-2028 is the result of the strategy session for the board and employees in 2024.
The finished product will be shaped by input from club members.
The adopted strategy document provides guidance for how the board plans and the administration runs the club.
On this basis, the board and administration prepare an annual work programme to be presented to the AGM.
The club needs engaged members with insight into the club's short-term and long-term priorities.
We hope this document increases engagement in the running and development of the club.
The board encourages input at member meetings, through a member survey and directly to the club leadership.
Help the board and the club become even better.

The board and employees
Voss Hang- and Paraglider Club 2024

## Position and target group

The club is for members who want to enjoy themselves in a flying community in Voss!

A well-resourced club that drives the development of adventure sports.

## Vision

Create safe and genuine joy in flying!

## Mission

### What is our task?

To facilitate good experiences in the air and among people.

## Tools

### Focus

1. Training and safety: locally in the club, centrally towards the section and federation.
2. Community and activity: a social and active environment through and beyond flying.
3. Organisation: well-functioning as a whole, with good facilities and sustainable finances.
4. Cooperation: To secure good conditions and development.
   Cooperation partners are Ekstremsportveko, Voss Folk High School, Voss Parachute Club, clubs in the section, clubs locally in Voss, as well as local partners.
5. Local community: an engaged and positive role rooted in the local community in Voss.
   Especially in matters important to sport and the environment.

## Values

### The club's values

- Inclusive environment
- Development
- Safety

Well-established values that shape operations and the environment give the club the ability to create exciting opportunities for everyone.

### The club's characteristics

- Considerate
- Unifying
- Curious

A typical club member is described as a playful bringer of joy.

### Our values

#### Focus on safety at every level

Consider people, risk, weather conditions and the local environment.

#### Inclusive environment

The club should bring members together through good follow-up and a varied range of activities.

#### Development

Be curious, have the ability to self-evaluate and be receptive to input and criticism.

## Promise and evidence

### Our promise

Active and satisfied pilots

### Evidence

- Well-functioning administration and facilities.
- A healthy culture.
- Quality in courses and follow-up.
- Annual programme plans that are followed up, as well as evaluation of goal achievement.

This gives a sense of ownership and belonging to the club.
