## Before you fly

Check weather, wind, airspace and local rules before every flight.
This wiki supplements your own assessment and current NLF/HPAC requirements.

- [Flying sites and local conditions](/en/flying/flying-sites-around-voss)
- [Local flying safety rules](/en/safety/local-flying-safety-rules)
- [Emergency procedures](/en/safety/safety-and-emergency-procedures)

## Wiki governance

The wiki is the club's living handbook and knowledge base.
Its content is organised by topic; it should not have a separate handbook page that duplicates the material.

> **TO RESOLVE:** The board should appoint an editor and page owners for the main subject areas.
> They should check that content is accurate, current and consistent with the bylaws, AGM decisions and current NIF/NLF rules.

Formal procedures and governance documents should show their approval status, approval date and responsible body.
Wiki.js/Git edit history shows when content changed; it does not replace the date of formal approval.
Use clear page titles, links between related topics, and Nynorsk as the primary language with English translations where useful.
Do not publish passwords, sensitive personal information or internal personnel documents.
