# Children, young people and safe sport

> **TO RESOLVE:** Confirm whether the club accepts minors in activities or courses. If so, publish procedures for guardians, responsible adults, safety, transport and incident contacts. Also make clear what guardians should know about activities, membership, insurance, volunteering and club policies.

The board should appoint responsible roles for youth activities and police-certificate procedures. Document who explains the police-certificate process, how applications and checks are handled, and how the information is protected. Check all procedures and requirements against current NIF/NLF rules before publication.

See the [safety group](/en/sikkerhet/tryggleiksgruppe) for air-sports safety roles. This page does not replace applicable safety or regulatory requirements.
