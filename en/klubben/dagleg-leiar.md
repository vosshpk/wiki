# Managing Director

The managing director runs daily operations within the bylaws, board decisions, applicable rules and delegated authority. The board is the managing director's supervisor and holds employer responsibility.

## Responsibilities and documents

- [Managing director job description](/en/klubben/dagleg-leiar/stillingsinstruks) describes duties, responsibilities and reporting.
- [Delegation matrix](/en/klubben/dagleg-leiar/fullmaktsmatrise) describes proposed authority and matters requiring board approval.

> **Important:** These documents were transcribed from submitted drafts. Adoption status and date are not recorded. The board must confirm the delegated limits before they are relied on.

## Items to resolve

- Current role holder and contact details.
- Board decision and minutes approving the job description and delegation matrix.
- Practical routines for staff matters, workplace safety, financial reporting and leave/absence.
