# Facilities and transport

Voss HPK has shared facilities for members, students and visiting pilots. Respect landowners, neighbours and other users. See the [club’s official facilities page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/fasilitetar-og-skulebakke/) for current information, prices and payment methods.

## Training hill at Bakkatun

The hill is used for courses and independent practice. See the [training hill page](/en/flysteder/skulebakke) for rules on access, the growing season, parking and respect for landowners.

## Clubhouse at Bømoen

The clubhouse is beside Voss Skydiving Club at Flyplassvegen 135. It is used for theory sessions, instructor meetings and club evenings.

- Members have access. Contact the club manager or operations contact for the current door code.
- Clean up after yourself, return equipment to its place, and close and lock doors and windows when leaving.

## Club trailer at Vangen

The trailer is by the main landing area at Prestegardslandet, beside Lake Vangsvatnet. It serves as a base for tandem equipment, first-aid supplies, courses and events. Clean up, use the bins and extinguish grills safely.

## Rescue boat

The orange rescue boat is kept on Lake Vangsvatnet and is available for emergencies or assistance. Read the [emergency procedures](/en/sikkerhet/nodprosedyrer) before using it; they include the safety routine for the engine and kill-switch lanyard.

## Club car and shared transport

The club car provides rides from landing areas to launch sites around Voss and nearby areas.

- Arrange trips in the club’s Facebook or Messenger groups.
- Drivers should enable [Volandoo live tracking](https://volandoo.com/clubs/voss-hpk/live/all).
- The driver coordinates payment. Pay the current fare and settle each trip promptly.
- Return the car with a full tank and a clean interior. Report faults or maintenance needs.

Route prices and payment details can change. Check the [official facilities page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/fasilitetar-og-skulebakke/) for current information.

## Aurland landing fee

A landing fee may apply at Høgåsen in Aurland. Check the [club’s official facilities page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/fasilitetar-og-skulebakke/) for the current rate and payment method before flying.
