# Club facilities, clubhouse and transport

Voss HPK has shared facilities for members, students and visiting pilots. Respect landowners, neighbours and other users. Check the [club’s official facilities page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/fasilitetar-og-skulebakke/) for current information.

## Training hill at Bakkatun

The training hill is used for ground handling and independent practice outside courses.

- The main area, marked **A** on the club’s map, is normally available. Reduce or stop activity when the grass is tall before mowing, and respect grazing animals and landowners.
- Areas **B–D** may only be used with prior agreement.
- Do not drive or park on the gravel road or in farmyards. Park in the designated area by Voss Skydiving Club and walk to the hill.
- Take all rubbish with you. If you are unsure whether the hill is open, contact the club manager at [malene@vosshpk.no](mailto:malene@vosshpk.no).

## Clubhouse at Bømoen

The clubhouse is beside Voss Skydiving Club at Flyplassvegen 135. It is used year-round for courses and meetings. Members have access; the door code changes periodically, so ask the operations contact or club manager for the current code. Keep the rooms clean, put equipment back, and close and lock doors and windows when leaving.

## Club trailer at Vangen

The trailer is by the main landing area at Prestegardslandet. It stores tandem equipment, payment terminals and first-aid supplies, and serves as a base during events such as Ekstremsportveko. Members may use the benches and grills nearby. Clean up after yourself and extinguish the grills safely.

## Rescue boat on Vangsvatnet

The orange rescue boat is kept ready on the lake and has no code lock. It is available for emergencies and assistance.

1. Start the engine in neutral.
2. Attach the kill-switch lanyard to the driver before moving.
3. Wear a life jacket or buoyancy aid.

## Club car and shared transport

The club car provides rides from landing areas to launch sites. Announce planned trips in the club’s Facebook group and Messenger community, and enable [Volandoo live tracking](https://volandoo.com/clubs/voss-hpk/live/all).

- The driver does not pay for a trip when there are more than two people in the car.
- The driver is responsible for ensuring that everyone pays. Settle the fare on the trip; do not leave unpaid rides.
- Return the car clean and with a full tank. Report faults or maintenance needs to the operations contact.

## Aurland landing fee

A landing fee applies at Høgåsen in Aurland. The club’s published options are NOK 10 per flight (maximum NOK 50 per day), NOK 100 for a long weekend, or NOK 300 for an annual pass. Pay using Vipps by searching for “Voss HPK Flyavgift Aurland” or use account **9581.05.18384**.
