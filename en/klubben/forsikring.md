# Insurance

Membership and licences may include insurance through NLF/NIF.
Coverage depends on activity, age, licence and policy terms.
Confirm coverage directly with the [NLF](https://nlf.no/) and insurer before flying or taking part in an activity.

> **TO RESOLVE:** The board should publish a current overview of insurance for members/pilots (including age, licence and activity), employees and volunteers, club equipment and facilities, events and trips, and any fidelity insurance for account signatories.

This page identifies topics to verify; it does not confirm insurance coverage.
