# Volunteering and roles

The club relies on volunteers. Volunteering must remain voluntary; tasks and expectations should be clear and assigned predictably.

> **TO RESOLVE:** Maintain an up-to-date list of volunteer tasks, owners, contacts and any reimbursement or expense coverage. Confirm which roles require election, appointment or specific training, and what agreement or membership is required for people taking on elected or ongoing responsibilities.

For air-sports training, see the [training section](/en/opplaering), the [board and committees](/en/styret), and the [safety group](/en/sikkerhet/tryggleiksgruppe). Club events and trips are covered under [activities](/en/klubben/aktivitetar).
