# Communications and privacy

The club uses its website, wiki, email and social media to communicate with members and others. See the [communications section](/en/kommunikasjon) for internal and external communications plans. Official contact points are listed on the [about page](/en/klubben/om-klubben).

> **TO RESOLVE:** The board should define channel owners, where formal information is published, how urgent notices are sent, and how personal data and photographs are handled.

Procedures should address consent before publishing photos, access to membership information, retention periods and a contact for questions. Do not put sensitive personal information in the wiki.

> **TO RESOLVE:** Confirm whether the club has a visual identity or logo guide, where it is stored, and who approves use of the logo and photographs. Also identify the authoritative channel for formal notices.
