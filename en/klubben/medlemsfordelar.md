# Member benefits

Members may have access to discounts, partner offers and practical benefits through the club and the Norwegian Air Sports Federation (NLF).

Offers, terms, discount codes and prices change. Check the [club’s current member-benefits overview](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/bli-medlem/) before booking or buying. Have a valid membership card or discount code ready if required. The NLF also publishes [member benefits and partner offers](https://nlf.no/om-nlf/medlemsfordeler-og-partnere/).

## Road fee to Gjelle

The road fee is covered for the club car. Private cars must pay the ordinary toll.
