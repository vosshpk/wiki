# Membership and benefits

Membership of Voss HPK connects you with the club and may provide access to shared facilities, transport arrangements and partner discounts.

## Join the club

- **Annual membership fee:** The club’s current website lists NOK 400 per year. Confirm the current fee before joining.
- **Join online:** [Register through MinIdrett](https://medlemskap.nif.no/Start/Index/25357).
- **Membership card:** Find the digital card in your MinIdrett profile or app. Some partner discounts require it.
- **Licence and insurance:** Independent flying also requires a valid licence and insurance through the [Norwegian Air Sports Federation (NLF)](https://nlf.no).

## Member benefits and discounts

Show a valid membership card or provide the applicable discount code. Terms and prices may change; confirm them with the provider and consult the [club’s current member-benefits page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/bli-medlem/).

### Voss Resort and Voss Gondola

The club publishes discounts for single rides and day passes bought online, discounts for course participants and instructors, and a kiosk discount at the lower gondola station. Members who decide not to launch because conditions are unsafe may be eligible for a return ride; ask gondola staff.

### Myrkdalen Mountain Resort

The club publishes member and course-participant offers for the Myrkdalsekspressen and ski passes. Confirm current prices, eligibility and purchase locations with the club or resort.

### Other partners

The club’s website lists offers from Skandinavisk Høyfjellsutstyr Voss, Peppes Pizza, Minigolfen café, Wild Sauna Gudvangen, MC-utstyr, Voss Bowling, Fleischer’s Motel and Ekstremsportveko. Current discounts, rates and codes are maintained on the [official benefits page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/bli-medlem/).

## Membership and flying safety

Members using club sites and shared facilities must follow current NLF/HPAC safety rules, [local Voss flying rules](/en/sikkerhet/flytrygging), and [emergency procedures](/en/sikkerhet/nodprosedyrer).

## Update or end membership

Update your contact details, address or membership through your personal [MinIdrett](https://minidrett.nif.no) profile.
