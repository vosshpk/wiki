# About Voss HPK

## About the club

Voss Hang- og Paragliderklubb (Voss HPK) was founded in 1976 and is one of Norway’s largest air sports clubs for hang gliding and paragliding. The high mountains of western Norway, flying conditions and a long-standing air-sports community make Voss a popular national and international free-flight destination throughout the year.

Voss Gondola and the launch sites on Hangurstoppen are central to flying activity. Main landing areas are Prestegardslandet and Minigolfen by Lake Vangsvatnet. The club also maintains launch sites for different wind directions and facilities at Bømoen.

## Activities

- **Paragliding and hang gliding:** Thermic, ridge and cross-country flying, as well as acro over Lake Vangsvatnet.
- **Speed riding and speed flying:** Mainly winter and spring flying on skis in Myrkdalen and Voss Resort.
- **Courses and training:** Beginner and progression courses, tandem check-outs and instructor training in cooperation with the Norwegian Air Sports Federation (NLF/HPAC).
- **Community and events:** Club gatherings, social evenings by the club trailer, Ekstremsportveko and Voss Open.

## Governance and management

The annual meeting is the club’s highest authority and elects the board. Day-to-day and sporting operations are handled by the club’s management and committees.

- **Managing director / acting technical lead:** See [managing director](/en/dagleg-leiar) for contact details and instructions.
- **Board:** Responsible for strategic and financial development. See the [board and committees](/en/styret).
- **Safety group:** Works with training, safety, rules, environmental matters and police certificates. See the [safety group page](/en/sikkerhet/tryggleiksgruppe).
- **Equipment and facilities:** See [club equipment and facilities](/en/klubbutstyr) for the clubhouse, trailer, rescue boat and club car. The training hill is included in the [flying-sites page](/en/fly/flysteder).
