# Accommodation for pilots and course participants

Voss HPK does not operate its own accommodation, but the club website lists the following options.
Availability, terms and prices can change; confirm details directly with the provider before booking.

## Voss Skydiving Club

Voss Skydiving Club offers bunkhouse accommodation and camping for vehicles and tents.
Availability, rates and payment methods can vary throughout the year.
Check the [Voss Skydiving Club website](https://www.skydivevoss.no/) and confirm details directly before travelling.
Voss HPK members may be eligible for a member rate.

## Voss Folk High School

Voss Folk High School offers summer accommodation with breakfast.
It is about a 10–15 minute walk from the main landing area and gondola.
See [booking and information](https://booking.voss.fhs.no/).

## Fleischer’s Motel

The club website may list member offers.
Check [Fleischer’s Motel](https://www.fleischers.no/) and confirm current rates, terms and availability directly before booking.

> Check the club’s [official accommodation information](https://vosshpk.no/kurs/praktisk-info-kurs/) for updates and further details. {.is-info}
