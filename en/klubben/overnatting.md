# Accommodation for pilots and course participants

Voss HPK does not operate its own accommodation, but the club website lists the following options. Availability, terms and prices can change; confirm details directly with the provider before booking.

## Voss Skydiving Club

Voss Skydiving Club offers bunkhouse accommodation and camping for vehicles and tents. Summer can be busy; call reception on **+47 56 51 10 00** to check availability. In winter, reception is not staffed regularly and payment for vehicle/tent stays is made through Vipps **89702**. Select “Accommodation” and pay the current amount. Voss HPK members receive a member rate. See [Voss Skydiving Club](https://www.skydivevoss.no/) for current terms.

## Voss Folk High School

Voss Folk High School offers summer accommodation with breakfast. It is about a 10–15 minute walk from the main landing area and gondola. See [booking and information](https://booking.voss.fhs.no/).

## Fleischer’s Motel

The club website lists an agreement for a four-person room near the main landing area for stays of at least seven nights. Published rates are NOK 1,200 per night from 1 October to 30 May and NOK 1,800 per night from 1 June to 30 September. Mention the Voss HPK agreement when booking and confirm current rates and availability with [Fleischer’s Motel](https://www.fleischers.no/).
