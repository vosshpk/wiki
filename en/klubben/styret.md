# Board and committees

The board is the club’s highest authority between annual meetings. The annual meeting elects the board, which is responsible for finances, operations, strategic direction and shared facilities.

Send matters, proposals and questions to [styret@vosshpk.no](mailto:styret@vosshpk.no).
Names and roles can change; see the [current board and committees on the club website](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/styre-og-utval/).
