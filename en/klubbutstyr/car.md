# Club Car and Transport

The club car provides rides from landing areas to launch sites around Voss and nearby areas.

- Arrange rides in the club's Facebook or Messenger groups.
- Drivers should enable [Volandoo live tracking](https://volandoo.com/clubs/voss-hpk/live/all).
- The driver coordinates payment. Pay the current fare and settle each trip promptly.
- Return the car with a full tank and a clean interior. Report faults or maintenance needs.
- Fares and payment details can change. Check the [official club facilities page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/fasilitetar-og-skulebakke/) before travelling.
