# Clubhouse and Facilities

Voss HPK has shared facilities for members, students and visiting pilots. Respect landowners, neighbours and other users. See the [official club facilities page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/fasilitetar-og-skulebakke/) for current terms, prices and practical details.

## Clubhouse at Bømoen

The clubhouse is beside Voss Skydiving Club at Flyplassvegen 135. It is used for theory sessions, instructor meetings and club evenings.

- Members have access. Contact the managing director or operations contact for the current door code.
- Clean up, return equipment to its place, and close and lock doors and windows when leaving.
