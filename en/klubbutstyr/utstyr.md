# Shared Flying Equipment

Voss HPK has shared training and club equipment for courses, events, tandem flying and member use.

## Storage

Club wings, training harnesses, reserve parachutes, helmets, radios and course materials are stored at the [clubhouse at Bømoen](/en/klubbutstyr/clubhouse).
Operational tandem and emergency gear, first-aid kits and payment terminals are kept in the [club trailer](/en/klubbutstyr/trailer).

## Borrowing and use

- **Who may borrow:** Students on approved club courses and members with a valid NLF licence, with approval from the technical lead, training officer or managing director.
- **Pre-flight inspection:** Check the equipment before use for holes or wear in the canopy and seams; damage to lines, risers and maillons; and the condition of carabiners, buckles and reserve pins.
- **Report problems:** Promptly tell the equipment manager or [safety group](mailto:sikkerheit@vosshpk.no) about hard landings, bush landings, moisture or damage.
  Mark damaged equipment and remove it from use.

## Reserve parachutes

Follow the intervals required by the manufacturer and current NLF rules.
The club arranges group repacking days before the spring and winter seasons.
Approved packers and instructors can provide guidance.
Record inspection and repacking dates in the logbook and equipment register.
