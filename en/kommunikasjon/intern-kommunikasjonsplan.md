# Internal communications plan

## Purpose

This plan supports reliable information flow, cooperation between members and the board, and active participation in the club.

Its aims are to:

- ensure members and volunteers receive important information in time
- build an open culture of cooperation and sharing experience
- encourage members to take part in activities and decision-making

## Audiences

- the board
- volunteers and elected officers
- active and non-active members
- instructors and course coordinators
- working groups and committees

## Channels

Channels should be easy to use and accessible to their intended audience. The board should confirm which channels the club actually uses and what information belongs in each.

### Digital channels

- **Collaboration tools:** Slack or Microsoft Teams are mentioned in the source document for project work, quick information sharing and discussion.
- **Members' social media group:** For informal messages, photos and stories.
- **Email:** For official announcements, board minutes and documentation.
- **Document storage:** Google Drive or Dropbox are mentioned for annual reports, safety procedures and bylaws.
- **Member portal:** Could provide course information, resources and equipment reservations if the club has this functionality.

### In-person channels

- member meetings for information, discussion and decisions
- a noticeboard at the clubhouse for local notices
- the annual general meeting for strategic decisions and reporting

## Measures for effective internal communications

### Clear roles

Clarify responsibilities for board members, committee chairs and volunteers. Make the organisation plan easy for members to find. This can reduce uncertainty and duplicated work.

### Regular updates

The source document proposes weekly project updates and monthly board emails about progress and upcoming activities. The board should set a realistic schedule and channel.

### Feedback

Enable two-way communication, for example through a digital or physical feedback box and open question sessions with the board. Clarify how feedback is received and followed up.

### Training and resources

Provide guidance for the digital tools the club actually uses. Keep guidance on safety, flying sites and equipment maintenance up to date.

### Transparent decisions

Share relevant board minutes with members and present strategic goals and plans at the annual general meeting. Respect privacy and matters that must be handled confidentially.

### Social and inclusive community

Consider informal gatherings, shared meals or trips, and a mentoring scheme for new members.

## Responsibilities

- **Communications lead:** Follows up on how well internal communications are working.
- **Board:** Ensures important information is shared in time.
- **Working groups and committees:** Keep the board and members updated on their work.

The board should appoint the people responsible and confirm these roles.

## Review

Possible measures include email responses, activity in collaboration tools, attendance at member meetings and social events, and member feedback. The source document proposes a review every six months. The board should assign responsibility and set a date.
