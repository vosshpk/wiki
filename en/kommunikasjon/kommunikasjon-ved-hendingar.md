# Communications during incidents

> **Important:** This page concerns information flow.
> It does not replace emergency procedures, the safety system or reporting requirements from NLF and public authorities.
> Always follow current procedures and prioritise life and health.

## Initial actions and internal notification

1. The person who discovers or is involved in an incident notifies the safety officer under the current procedure.
2. The safety officer starts the report and contacts relevant people in line with the safety system.
3. The safety officer considers contacting the insurer early, in accordance with current insurance and reporting procedures.
4. Notify the safety group so it can support assessment and follow-up.
5. Inform the head of training when operational safety measures or wider learning are needed.
6. Inform the board of incidents with possible legal, financial or organisational consequences.

The order and responsibilities must follow the club's current safety system and emergency procedures.

## External notifications

- With the safety officer, the head of training considers submitting a report to NLF when required by the safety system or reporting rules.
- For serious incidents, responsible people must follow current requirements to notify emergency services, NLF and relevant authorities.
  The source document mentions the Norwegian Civil Aviation Authority; confirm the current process and responsible role before use.
- Agree on one spokesperson for media and public enquiries.
  Do not speculate or share unverified details.

## Information for members

When it is appropriate and the person responsible for communication has been agreed, the board or head of training may share necessary information with members.
Consider an information meeting or written update.
The safety group or head of training may call an extra safety meeting if the incident shows a need.

Do not share names, health information or other personal data without a proper basis and approval.
Do not share information publicly until the facts and the needs of those involved have been considered.

## Follow-up and learning

- Hold a debrief with those involved when appropriate.
- Record lessons and improvement proposals in the appropriate system, restricting access where needed.
- Update the safety system and procedures when changes are approved.
- Communicate approved measures to the people who need the information.

## Template for an initial message to the board

Use an approved channel.
Share as little personal information as possible.

> A serious incident occurred at [place/time].
> Emergency services have been [notified / not required / status].
> The extent of injuries is [unknown / brief confirmed status].
> We are supporting those involved and following the current notification procedures.
> Please do not share this further or speculate.
> We will provide another update when we have confirmed information.
