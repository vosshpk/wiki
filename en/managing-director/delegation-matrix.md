## Using the matrix effectively

1. **Adapt it to the club's needs:** The matrix should reflect the sports club's size, finances and complexity.
2. **Secure board approval:** The board must approve the matrix and record its decision in the minutes.
3. **Communicate clearly:** Ensure the managing director and other relevant people understand the limits of the delegation.
4. **Review as needed:** Review the matrix regularly, for example annually or when operations change.

## Special considerations

- **Budget responsibility:** The managing director must work within the budget approved by the annual general meeting, in accordance with section 13 of the club's bylaws.
- **Reporting:** The board requires regular reports from the managing director to maintain oversight of finances and activities.
- **Safety:** All decisions concerning flying, equipment and courses must comply with NLF rules and NIF laws on safety and responsibility.
- **Safety assessment for purchases:** When assessing safety aspects of purchases, include the safety group's assessment in the decision.
- **Board approval:** The board must approve the matrix and record its decision in the minutes.
- **Communication:** The managing director and other relevant people must understand the limits of the delegation.
- **Review:** The board should review the matrix regularly, for example annually or when operations, organisation, the budget or areas of responsibility change.
- **Financial limits:** Clarify how the limits are calculated for multi-year contracts and whether amounts include VAT.
- **Hiring temporary employees:** The source document gives the managing director authority to initiate hiring, but also says that the board decides.
  The board must clarify the mandate, approved hiring plan and staffing budget/strategy.
