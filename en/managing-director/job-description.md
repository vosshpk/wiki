# Job description for the managing director of Voss HPK

> **Status:** Transcribed from “Stillingsinstruks DL 2024”.
> No approval date is provided.
> This is a working document for board confirmation, not proof of current employment terms.

**Title:** Managing director<br>
**Reports to:** Board of Voss Hang- og Paragliderklubb (Voss HPK)<br>
**Purpose:** Ensure the responsible operation and development of Voss HPK in line with the club's bylaws, the Norwegian Sports Federation's (NIF) laws and the club's strategic goals.
The managing director shall safeguard the club's interests, create a good environment for all members and ensure sound administrative and financial management.

## 1. Main duties

The managing director has overall responsibility for the club's day-to-day operations and development.
This includes the following main areas:

### 1.1 Administration

- **Ensure compliance with bylaws and rules:** Ensure that Voss HPK operates at all times in accordance with the club's bylaws, NIF rules and other applicable legislation for sports organisations.
- **Follow up decisions:** Prepare matters for board meetings, keep minutes and ensure follow-up of decisions made by the board and annual general meeting.
- **Membership administration:** Ensure that the membership register is up to date and accurate in line with NIF requirements, including joining, leaving and follow-up of membership fees.
- **Communications:** Be responsible for the club's website, social media and other information flow, and ensure prompt and accurate follow-up of emails and other enquiries.

### 1.2 Financial management

- **Financial responsibility:** Manage the club's funds within the adopted budget and delegation matrix, and be responsible for day-to-day financial operations in cooperation with the treasurer and any auditor.
- **Budget and accounts:** Prepare a proposed annual budget and ensure regular financial reporting to the board.
- **Funding applications:** Prepare applications for grants from public and private organisations, such as NIF, Voss Herad (Voss Municipality), local organisations and sponsors.
- **Membership fees:** Follow up payments of membership fees and other income.

### 1.3 Equipment operations and maintenance

- **Equipment:** Ensure that the club's air-sports equipment and other property are properly maintained, comply with applicable requirements and are insured.
- **Purchases:** Purchase necessary equipment in line with the budget and needs.
- **Loans and use:** Ensure that the club's guidelines for loans, rentals and use of equipment are followed.

### 1.4 Planning and delivery of activities

- **Activity calendar:** Prepare and maintain an activity calendar for club events such as competitions, courses and social gatherings.
- **Volunteer work:** Plan and coordinate volunteer work together with the board and volunteers.
- **Safety:** Ensure that the club's activities comply with current safety rules from the Norwegian Civil Aviation Authority and NIF guidelines.
- **Projects:** Initiate and deliver projects in accordance with the strategy.

### 1.5 Staff management and volunteer coordination

- **Responsibility for employees:** Follow up any employees, including work allocation, training and feedback.
- **Volunteers:** Coordinate and motivate volunteers, and ensure that they have clear tasks and sufficient training.

### 1.6 Sponsorship and cooperation

- **Sponsorship agreements:** Follow up the club's sponsors and ensure that agreements are carried out.
- **Cooperation:** Maintain and develop cooperation with relevant organisations such as the Norwegian Air Sports Federation, local businesses and public bodies.
  Represent the club at meetings and in networks.

## 2. Authority

The managing director has authority to:

- **Operational duties:** Make decisions about the club's day-to-day operations, including financial transactions within the adopted budget and delegation matrix.
- **Use of funds:** Make purchases and enter into agreements in accordance with the board's guidelines.
- **Personnel responsibility:** Follow up work tasks and training for employees and volunteers.

## 3. Reporting and cooperation

- **Reporting:** The managing director reports to the board and must provide regular updates on finances, operations and activities.
- **Cooperation:** Work closely with the board, safety group, members, sponsors and other partners in the best interests of the club.
- **Annual report:** Prepare an annual report for presentation to the annual general meeting.

## 4. Qualifications and personal qualities

### 4.1 Qualifications

- Experience in administration and financial management, preferably in a sports club or voluntary organisation.
- Knowledge of NIF rules and the organisation of sport in Norway.
- Good digital skills, including use of membership and accounting systems.
- Experience planning and delivering events.

### 4.2 Personal qualities

- Strong communication and cooperation skills.
- Organised, solution-oriented and self-reliant.
- Committed and motivated to further develop the club.
- Able to handle several tasks at once and prioritise effectively.

## 5. Working hours and terms

- **Working hours:** Flexible hours, including evenings and weekends when required for events, courses or meetings.
- **Pay and employment terms:** Set by the board and stated in the employment agreement.
  Otherwise, the Norwegian Working Environment Act applies.

The employment agreement and applicable law take precedence over this general instruction where they regulate a matter in more detail.

## 6. Review of the job description

The board shall review this job description annually in cooperation with the managing director to ensure that it is suited to the club's needs.
