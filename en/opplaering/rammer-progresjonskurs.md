# Progression course framework

*Source: “Retningslinjer for progresjonskurs Voss HPK”, dated 10 June 2024.*

## General framework

- Every course must have at least two instructors.
- The lead instructor must check that participants meet the entry requirements and that their equipment complies with the rules and is suitable for them.
- Do as much ground handling as possible, in addition to training on the training hill and high flights. This applies at every level.
- Continue to focus on a sound flight plan with generous margins when practising new skills.

## Training hill and equipment

Training and flying on the training hill should also serve as an equipment check for instructors. Check safety and suitability, including size, trim and characteristics. Continue training until each participant has completed several takeoffs and landings with good technique.

## Launch technique and ground handling

- Use a steady pull-up, check the wing and accelerate gradually towards maximum speed.
- Participants should be able to hold their planned line and stop before the “point of no return”.
- Use good technique; do not brake the wing into lift with the brakes.
- Practise forward and reverse ground handling in wind. Participants should balance the wing overhead and be able to bring it down under control.

## High flights

- Give a thorough briefing on landing and launch. Choose the landing area with the best margin. At Voss, use the Ringheim landing area; it gives more margin and helps students retain capacity for their first landing without skis.
- Conditions for foot launching should be optimal: wind straight in at 2–3 m/s, little thermal activity and stable conditions.
- Students must be clearly above the minimum standard to pass. As a rule of thumb, complete at least three high flights, including at least one forward launch and one reverse launch. This gives participants the best chance to learn in a real launch setting.

## Risk assessment and review

- Discuss risk assessment. Take time with all participants to assess the day's conditions before flying.
- Debrief afterwards and identify learning points.
- Create a separate list of equipment well suited to foot launching early in progression.

## SPG instructor-section review

The source calls for a meeting with SPG instructors to review the section and gather suggestions for improvement. The season is year-round.
