# Wiki governance

The wiki is the club's living handbook and knowledge base. It organises information, procedures and governance documents by topic; it does not need a separate “club handbook” page duplicating that content.

## Ownership and review

> **TO RESOLVE:** The board should appoint an editor and page owners for key subject areas. Owners should check that content is accurate, current and consistent with bylaws, AGM decisions and current NIF/NLF rules.

Formal procedures and governance documents should state adoption status, adoption date and responsible body. Wiki.js/Git history shows when content was edited; it does not replace the date of formal approval.

Use clear page titles, link related topics, and use Nynorsk as the main language with English translations where useful. Do not publish passwords, sensitive personal information or internal personnel records.
