# Bylaws of Voss Hang- og Paragliderklubb

> This is an English translation of the club's bylaws.
> The Nynorsk version is the authoritative text; if there is any discrepancy, the Nynorsk version prevails.

Founded 1 April 1974.

Last revised by the board on 27 April 2026 under authority granted by a resolution of the annual meeting on 6 March 2026.

These bylaws follow the standard bylaws of the Norwegian Olympic and Paralympic Committee and Confederation of Sports (NIF), effective from 1 January 2026.

## § 1 Purpose

The purpose of the sports club is to engage in sports organized under the Norwegian Olympic and Paralympic Committee and Confederation of Sports (NIF).
The club's work shall be characterized by voluntary effort, democracy, loyalty and equality.
All sporting activity shall be based on the values adopted by the Sports Assembly.

## § 2 Organization

(1) The sports club is self-governing and independent, and has only individual members.

(2) The sports club is a member of the Norwegian Air Sports Federation, in the Hang-, Para- and Speedgliding Section.
The rules on the club's obligation to belong to a sport-specific federation are set out in NIF's bylaws [§ 10-1 (4)](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-10-idrettslag/#%C2%A710-1).

(3) The sports club is a member of NIF and is therefore affiliated with Vestland Sports Confederation.
It is based in Voss Municipality and is a member of Voss Sports Council.

(4) The rules on the right of representation are set out in NIF's bylaws [§ 10-3 (1)](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-10-idrettslag/#%C2%A710-3).

(5) The rules requiring the sports club to comply with the regulations and decisions of superior organizational bodies are set out in NIF's bylaws [§§ 2-2](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-2) and [2-3](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-3).

## § 3 Members

(1) The rules on admission of members, resignation, expulsion and similar matters are set out in NIF's bylaws §§ [10-4](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-10-idrettslag/#%C2%A710-4) and [10-6](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-10-idrettslag/#%C2%A710-6).

(2) The club's duty to register information in the sports membership and organization register is governed by the [Regulations on the Sports Membership and Organization Register](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/regelverk/forskrift-om-idrettens-medlems--og-organisasjonsregister/), cf. NIF's bylaws [§ 10-5](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-10-idrettslag/#%C2%A710-5).

## § 4 Gender composition

The rules on gender composition on boards and committees, and when representing the club at meetings of superior organizational bodies, are set out in NIF's bylaws [§ 2-4](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-4).
For sports clubs whose turnover and membership are [below the threshold set by the NIF Board](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/lovnormer/for-idrettslag/terskel/), it is sufficient for both genders to be represented on boards and committees and when representing the club at meetings of superior organizational bodies.

## § 10 The annual meeting and bodies elected by it

(1) The annual meeting is the sports club's highest governing body and is held each year by the end of March.

(2) Ordinary and extraordinary annual meetings are held in accordance with these bylaws and NIF's bylaws [§§ 2-15](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-15), [2-16](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-16), [2-17](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-17), [2-19](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-19) and [2-20](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-20).

(3) The duties of the annual meeting are to:

1. Approve those entitled to vote
2. Elect the meeting chair(s)
3. Elect the minute-taker(s)
4. Elect two members to sign the minutes
5. Approve the rules of procedure
6. Approve the notice of the meeting
7. Approve the agenda
8. Consider the sports club's annual report
9. Consider
    1. the sports club's accounts
    2. the board's financial report
    3. any report from the control committee
    4. any report from an appointed auditor
10. Consider other matters included in the approved agenda
11. Set
    1. the membership fee, which must be at least NOK 50
    2. any training fee, or authorize the board to set training fees
12. Adopt the sports club's budget
13. Consider the sports club's organizational plan
14. If the sports club's turnover and membership are above the [threshold set by the NIF Board](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/lovnormer/for-idrettslag/terskel/), elect:
    1. a board consisting of a chair, deputy chair, three board members and two deputy members
    2. an audit committee with a chair and at least one member
    3. representatives to meetings of organizational bodies where the sports club has the right to representation, or authorize the board to appoint those representatives
    4. a nomination committee with a chair, one member and one deputy member
    5. any other positions in accordance with the sports club's organizational plan

    If the sports club's turnover and membership are below the [threshold set by the NIF Board](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/lovnormer/for-idrettslag/terskel/), elect:
    1. a board consisting of a chair, deputy chair, three board members and two deputy members
    2. representatives to meetings of organizational bodies where the sports club has the right to representation, or authorize the board to appoint those representatives
    3. any other positions in accordance with the sports club's organizational plan
15. Decide, if applicable, whether to appoint an auditor to audit the sports club's accounts

Chairs and deputy chairs shall be elected individually.
The other members shall be elected together.
Deputy members shall then be elected together.
If more than one deputy member is elected, they shall be designated first deputy member, second deputy member, and so on.

The rules on voting at the annual meeting are set out in NIF's bylaws [§ 2-21](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-21).

(4) If the sports club has a control committee, NIF's bylaws [§ 2-12](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-12) apply.
The annual meeting may adopt instructions for the control committee.

(5) If the sports club has a nomination committee, NIF's bylaws [§ 2-18](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-18) apply.
The annual meeting may adopt instructions for the nomination committee.

## § 11 The board of the sports club

(1) The sports club is managed and bound by the board, which represents the sports club externally.

(2) The board is the sports club's highest authority between annual meetings, but certain decisions may only be adopted by the annual meeting in accordance with § 10 (3) and NIF's bylaws [§ 2-22](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-22).

(3) The board is responsible for the administration and oversight of all parts of the sports club's activities.
The board shall ensure:
- that the purpose of the sports club is fulfilled
- that the sports club's activities and financial management are organized responsibly, including assessing the sports club's insurance needs and taking out the necessary insurance
- that decisions are made in accordance with the regulations and decisions of superior organizational bodies, the sports club's bylaws and resolutions of the annual meeting
- that information is registered in accordance with § 3 (2)
- that a person responsible for the police certificate scheme is appointed if the sports club organizes sports for minors or people with intellectual disabilities
- that a person responsible for children's sports is appointed if the sports club organizes children's sports

(4) The board may appoint committees or working groups to handle ongoing or one-off tasks, and may prepare their mandates or instructions.

(5) The board shall appoint representatives to meetings of superior organizational bodies if the annual meeting has not elected representatives.

(6) The board shall meet when the chair decides, or when at least two board members request a meeting.

(7) The Technical Director (Fagleg leiar) is appointed by the section and is governed by the [HPS Safety System](https://nlf.no/grener/hps/sikkerhet--utdanning/sikkerhetssystemet/).
The Technical Director establishes and runs the safety group, which may have its own safety officer.

## § 12 Groups

(1) The annual meeting may decide to establish or dissolve groups and determine how they are organized and managed, or delegate this authority to the board.

(2) If the annual meeting has decided, when considering the organizational plan, to establish groups with group boards, the following applies to sports clubs whose turnover and membership are [above the threshold set by the NIF Board](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/lovnormer/for-idrettslag/terskel/):

1. Each group shall have a group board of at least three members.
   The group board is elected at the annual meeting or appointed by the board under authority delegated by the annual meeting.
2. The group should hold at least one annual meeting to discuss the group's finances and activities, propose candidates for the group board, and provide any input on matters considered by the board or annual meeting.
3. The group board constitutes itself unless the annual meeting or the board, under authority delegated by the annual meeting, decides otherwise.
4. The group board sets any annual training fee within the authority granted by the annual meeting or the board.

(3) A group board or group representative may not enter into agreements or otherwise bind the sports club without authorization from the main board or the annual meeting, and must act within the limits set by the board or annual meeting.

## § 13 Amendments

The rules on amendments to these bylaws are set out in NIF's bylaws [§ 2-2](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-2-felles-bestemmelser-for-hele-organisasjonen/#%C2%A72-2).

## § 14 Dissolution, withdrawal, etc.

(1) The sports club may resolve to dissolve:

1. by a unanimous resolution to dissolve at the annual meeting; or
2. by adopting a resolution to dissolve by a two-thirds majority at two consecutive annual meetings, where the subsequent meeting must be held at least three months and no more than six months later.

For a resolution to dissolve to be valid, the sports club must notify the sports district and the sport-specific federation in writing no later than 14 days before the annual meeting at which the dissolution proposal is considered.

(2) A merger with another sports club is not considered a dissolution of the sports club.
A resolution to merge or withdraw from a sport-specific federation, and any necessary amendments to these bylaws in connection with this, must be adopted by a two-thirds majority of the annual meeting.

(3) The rules on withdrawal from NIF and loss of NIF membership are set out in NIF's bylaws [§ 10-2](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/tema/juss/nifs-lov/kapittel-10-idrettslag/#%C2%A710-2).
