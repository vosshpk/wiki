# Financial procedures

The board has overall responsibility for the prudent use and management of club funds. Delegated authority for the managing director is described in the [delegation matrix](/en/dagleg-leiar/fullmaktsmatrise). Bylaws and board decisions must be followed.

> **TO RESOLVE:** Publish an approved procedure assigning responsibility for budgets, bookkeeping, invoices, expenses, bank access, payments, reconciliation, reporting and records. Confirm the two-person/two-signature controls and any fidelity insurance.

The procedure should identify the bookkeeper/treasurer and explain how the board reviews accounts and budgets. Do not publish account numbers, passwords or personal financial information in the wiki.
