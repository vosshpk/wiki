# Voss HPK bylaws

This page summarizes the club's bylaws. The source text is in Norwegian Nynorsk, which remains authoritative. The bylaws were last amended on 8 March 2024. Check that this is the current version and follow the applicable NIF rules before formal decisions.

The bylaws cover the club's purpose and affiliations; membership; representation and eligibility; conflicts of interest, voting and meeting procedure; accounts and audit; the annual meeting and its responsibilities; the board and committees; amendments; and dissolution.

The annual meeting is the club's highest governing body and is held each year by the end of March. Its responsibilities include approving reports and accounts, setting membership fees, adopting the budget and organizational plan, and electing the board and other roles specified by the bylaws and applicable NIF rules.
