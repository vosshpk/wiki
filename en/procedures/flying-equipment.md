## Inspection and maintenance

Create an inventory recording owner, serial number, use, condition and next inspection.
Set pre-use/loan checks and periodic inspections according to manufacturer instructions, NLF/HPS rules and qualification requirements.
Remove equipment from use if it is damaged, has an unknown history or its airworthiness is in doubt until a qualified person has assessed it.
