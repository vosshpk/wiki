# Roles and areas of responsibility

> **Status:** This is an overview of roles the club should clarify.
> It does not confirm that the roles have been established, who holds them or what authority they have.
> The board and technical director must define the mandate, reporting line, required qualifications and any compensation for each role.

> **To be confirmed:** The descriptions of the board, managing director, technical director and safety group, especially statements about authority, come from a role clarification and do not confirm current delegations.
> Check them against the bylaws, organisation plan, safety system, and instructions for the board, managing director and technical director before relying on them.

The source document is a clarification of communications and authority for the board, managing director, technical director and safety group of Voss Hang- og Paragliderklubb.

## Board

**Responsibilities:**

- Overall legal, financial and organisational responsibility for the club.
- Ensure that the club follows applicable laws, regulations such as BSL D 4-7, and internal guidelines.
- Approve budgets, strategies and major measures relating to safety and training.
- Facilitate good cooperation between the managing director, technical director and safety group.

**Communications:**

- The board receives reports from the managing director on operations and finances, and from the technical director on safety and training work.
- Ensure open dialogue with the safety group through the technical director on relevant matters.

**Authority described in the source document:**

- Make final decisions on major financial and strategic measures.
- May override professional decisions if legal or financial concerns arise.

> **To be confirmed:** Check the description of authority against current rules and the safety system before relying on it.

## Managing director

**Responsibilities:**

- Overall responsibility for the club's day-to-day operations, including administration, finances and member contact.
- Ensure the resources required for safety and training work are available.
- Coordinate between the board, technical director and safety group to achieve efficient operations.

**Communications:**

- Report to the board on finances, membership trends and practical challenges.
- Work closely with the technical director to ensure safety is integrated into day-to-day operations.
- Inform the safety group about resources and operational limits.

**Authority described in the source document:**

- Make decisions relating to operations and use of resources within the approved financial limits.
- Initiate measures that support safety work in consultation with the technical director.

See also the [delegation of authority matrix](/en/managing-director/delegation-matrix) and the [managing director page](/en/managing-director/managing-director).

## Tandem lead

Follows up tandem activities from a professional and organisational perspective in accordance with current NLF/HPS rules and club decisions.
Works with the technical director, tandem coordinator and tandem pilots.
**To be clarified:** approval authority, reporting, records and the distinction from the technical director's responsibilities.

## Tandem coordinator

Coordinates the practical delivery of tandem activities, including planning, staffing, booking flow, equipment and information sharing.
Follows current safety and privacy procedures.
**To be clarified:** mandate, system access and cooperation with the managing director and tandem lead.

## Ekstremsportveko discipline lead — speed

Plans and coordinates the club's speed-riding discipline during Ekstremsportveko, including practical preparations, volunteers, participant information and contact with the organiser.
Follows current safety requirements and the event's conditions.
**To be clarified:** budget, decision authority and reporting.

## Ekstremsportveko discipline lead — paragliding

Plans and coordinates the paragliding discipline during Ekstremsportveko, including activities, staffing, participant information and contact with the organiser.

**To be clarified:** budget, decision authority and reporting.

## Course lead

Coordinates the course calendar, instructors, participant follow-up, venues and equipment.
Works with the technical director on professional requirements and with the managing director on administration, payments and information.
Use the current [course delivery framework](/en/instructor-information/course-framework) and [progression course framework](/en/instructor-information/progression-course-framework).
**To be clarified:** who approves courses and instructors.

## Technical director

**Responsibilities:**

- Operational leadership of safety and training work.
- Implement and maintain the club's safety system and regulations.
- Follow up and approve instructors, tandem pilots and courses in accordance with the club's and NLF's guidelines.

**Communications:**

- Work with the managing director on resources and needs for courses and safety measures.
- Inform the board about the status of and measures relating to safety and training.
- Hold regular meetings with the safety group to assess risk and operational measures.

**Authority described in the source document:**

- Stop activities or courses when the risk is unacceptable.
- Make professional decisions without consulting the board, as long as they do not have major financial consequences.
- Ensure that instructors and members follow safety standards.

> **To be confirmed:** Clarify the limits of this role against the current safety system, appointment, reporting line and delegations.

## Safety group

**Responsibilities:**

- Support the technical director in developing and carrying out safety procedures.
- Monitor the club's activities and propose measures to reduce risk.
- Evaluate safety incidents and report to the technical director and board.

**Communications:**

- Report deviations or safety incidents directly to the technical director.
- Work with the managing director on resources that affect safety.
- Give recommendations to the board on strategic matters.

**Authority described in the source document:**

- Together with the technical director, the group may stop activities considered dangerous.
- Give recommendations to the board and managing director on safety-related measures.

> **To be confirmed:** Confirm this authority against the safety system.

### Safety officer

- Support the technical director and safety group with safety work, risk assessments, follow-up of observations and sharing learning points.
- Follow the NLF/HPS safety system.
- Do not take over responsibilities held by the technical director.

> **To be clarified:** Mandate, reporting and boundaries with the safety group.

### Cooperation and decisions

1. **The board** has overall responsibility and sets strategic direction.
2. **The managing director** ensures efficient day-to-day operations and that resources support safety work.
3. **The technical director** leads operational safety work and cooperates with the managing director to put safety into practice.
4. **The safety group** is an independent support function that assists the technical director and advises the board and managing director.

### Practical example of the division of responsibilities

- In the event of a safety incident, the technical director and safety group may stop activities immediately.
  The managing director handles practical matters and allocates resources.
  The board makes long-term decisions about measures to prevent similar situations.
- See [communications during incidents](/en/working-documents/communications-during-incidents) and the [emergency procedures](/en/safety/emergency-procedures).

## Volunteer-work lead

Plans volunteer work, clarifies tasks and necessary equipment, assigns work and follows up to ensure areas and equipment are tidied afterwards.
Works with the managing director and the people responsible for the activities.
**To be clarified:** how volunteer work is requested, announced and documented.

## Police-certificate scheme lead

Administers the club's police-certificate scheme under current requirements by informing relevant people, following up the application and checking process, and handling documentation confidentially.
**To be clarified:** who may access what, secure storage and a deputy.
Follow NIF's current procedures.

## Equipment manager

Keeps an overview of the club's equipment, identification, loans, condition and maintenance needs.
Coordinates inspections with qualified people and reports purchase or replacement needs to the managing director or relevant roles.
See the [flying-equipment routine](/en/procedures/flying-equipment).
**To be clarified:** equipment scope, inspection qualifications and purchasing authority.

## Car manager

Follows up the club car, including servicing, tyres, cleaning, records and reporting faults.
See the [car routine](/en/procedures/car).
**To be clarified:** who books servicing, approves costs and keeps track of keys and vehicle requirements.

## Boat manager

Follows up the tow boat and rescue boats, including condition, mooring, safety equipment, haul-out, servicing and records.
Works with the technical director on safety.
See the [boat and mooring routine](/en/procedures/boats-and-moorings).
**To be clarified:** qualification requirements, service responsibility and emergency procedures.
