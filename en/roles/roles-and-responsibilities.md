# Roles and responsibilities

> **Status:** This page lists roles for the club to clarify.
> It does not confirm that a role has been established, who holds it or what authority it carries.
> The board and technical director should define each role's mandate, reporting line, required qualifications and any compensation.

> **To confirm:** The descriptions of the board, managing director, technical director and safety group, especially statements about authority, come from a role description and do not confirm current delegations. Check them against the bylaws, organisation plan, safety system, and instructions for the board, managing director and technical director before relying on them.

## Board

**Responsibilities:** Overall legal, financial and organisational responsibility.
The board should ensure the club follows applicable requirements and internal policies, approve budgets, strategy and major measures, and support cooperation between the managing director, technical director and safety group.

**Communications:** Receive reports from the managing director on operations and finances, and from the technical director on safety and training.
Maintain dialogue with the safety group through the technical director.

**Authority described in the source:** Make final decisions on major financial and strategic measures.
The source says the board may override professional decisions where legal or financial concerns arise. Check this wording against current rules and the safety system before relying on it.

## Managing director

**Responsibilities:** Day-to-day operations, including administration, finances and member contact.
Ensure safety and training work has the necessary resources, and coordinate between the board, technical director and safety group.

**Communications:** Report to the board on finances, membership trends and practical challenges.
Work with the technical director to integrate safety into daily operations and inform the safety group about resources and operating limits.

**Authority described in the source:** Make operational and resource decisions within approved financial limits, and initiate safety measures in consultation with the technical director.
See also the [delegation of authority matrix](/en/managing-director/delegation-matrix) and the [managing director page](/en/managing-director/managing-director).

## Tandem lead

Follows up the tandem activity from a technical and organisational perspective under current NLF/HPS rules and club decisions.
Works with the technical director, tandem coordinator and tandem pilots.
**To clarify:** approval authority, reporting, records and how this role differs from the technical director's responsibilities.

## Tandem coordinator

Coordinates practical tandem operations: planning, staffing, booking flow, equipment and information.
Follows current safety and privacy procedures.
**To clarify:** mandate, system access and coordination with the managing director and tandem lead.

## Ekstremsportveko speed lead

Plans and coordinates the club's speed-riding activity during Ekstremsportveko, including preparations, volunteers, participant information and contact with the event organiser.
Follows current safety requirements and event conditions.
**To clarify:** budget, decision authority and reporting.

## Ekstremsportveko paragliding lead

Plans and coordinates the paragliding activity during Ekstremsportveko, including activities, staffing, participant information and contact with the event organiser.
Coordinates safety, logistics and club resources.
**To clarify:** budget, decision authority and reporting.

## Course lead

Coordinates the course calendar, instructors, participant follow-up, venues and equipment.
Works with the technical director on professional requirements and with the managing director on administration, payment and information.
Uses the current [course delivery framework](/en/training/course-delivery-framework) and [progression course framework](/en/training/progression-course-framework).
**To clarify:** who approves courses and instructors.

## Technical director

**Responsibilities:** Operational leadership of safety and training, and implementation and maintenance of the club's safety system and rules.
Responsible for the technical functions of the air-sports activity under current NLF/HPS rules and safety systems. Follows up the safety group, training, instructors, tandem pilots, courses and technical procedures, and raises safety matters with the relevant bodies.

**Communications:** Work with the managing director on resources and training and safety needs.
Update the board on safety and training, and meet with the safety group to review risks and operational measures.

**Authority described in the source:** Stop activities or courses when risk is unacceptable, make professional decisions within the role, and ensure instructors and members follow safety standards.
Confirm these limits against the current safety system.
**To clarify:** current appointment, reporting line and authority.

## Safety group

**Responsibilities:** Support the technical director in developing and carrying out safety procedures, monitor activities, propose ways to reduce risk and evaluate incidents.

**Communications:** Report deviations and incidents to the technical director, work with the managing director on resources that affect safety, and advise the board on strategic matters.

**Authority described in the source:** Work with the technical director to stop activities considered dangerous and advise the board and managing director.
Confirm this against the safety system.

## Volunteer-work lead

Plans volunteer work, clarifies tasks and equipment needs, assigns work and follows up that areas and equipment are left tidy.
Coordinates with the managing director and activity owners.
**To clarify:** how work parties are requested, announced and recorded.

## Police-certificate scheme lead

Administers the club's police-certificate scheme under current requirements: informs relevant people, follows the application and check process, and handles documentation confidentially.
**To clarify:** access rights, secure storage and a deputy.
Follow current NIF procedures.

## Equipment manager

Maintains an overview of club equipment, identification, loans, condition and maintenance needs.
Coordinates inspections with qualified people and reports purchases or replacement needs to the managing director/board.
See the [flying-equipment routine](/en/procedures/flying-equipment).
**To clarify:** equipment scope, inspection qualifications and purchasing authority.

## Car manager

Follows up the club car, including servicing, tyres, cleaning, records and reporting faults.
See the [car routine](/en/procedures/car).
**To clarify:** who books service, approves costs and manages keys and vehicle requirements.

## Boat manager

Follows up the tow boat and rescue boats: condition, moorings, safety equipment, haul-out, servicing and records.
Works with the technical director on safety.
See the [boats and moorings routine](/en/procedures/boats-and-moorings).
**To clarify:** qualifications, service responsibility and emergency procedures.

## Safety officer

Supports the technical director and safety group with safety work, risk assessments, follow-up of observations and sharing lessons learned.
Follows the NLF/HPS safety system and does not replace responsibilities held by the technical director.
**To clarify:** mandate, reporting and boundaries with the safety group.

## Cooperation and decisions

- The board has overall responsibility and sets strategic direction.
- The managing director follows up day-to-day operations and resources.
- The technical director leads operational safety and training work.
- The safety group supports the technical director and advises the board and managing director.

For a safety incident, the source describes the technical director and safety group as able to stop activities, the managing director as handling practical needs and resources, and the board as considering longer-term measures.
See [communications during incidents](/en/working-documents/communications-during-incidents) and the [emergency procedures](/en/safety/emergency-procedures).
