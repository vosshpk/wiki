# Hang-, para- or speedglider accident response plan

This is an English translation of the accident response plan.
The source has no revision date.
Check the current NLF notification plan before relying on contact details.

## First aid and notifications

If you are first at the accident scene:

1. Start first aid.
   If the person is not breathing normally, begin CPR.
   Stop bleeding.
   If the person is breathing normally but unconscious, observe their breathing for one minute, then place them in the recovery position while waiting for medical personnel.
2. Call **113** as soon as possible for medical assistance.
3. Stay with the person until rescue personnel arrive and take over.
4. If the police have not been notified by 113, call **112** to report the accident.
5. Notify the Hang-, Para- og Speedglider section on **+47 908 67 560**.
   Follow the notification plan below.
6. Refer press enquiries to the section's chief officer.
   Do not speculate about the cause of the incident.
7. In the event of a fatality, the police contact the next of kin.

## Notification plan

The source document says the full notification plan, including names and phone numbers, is published on the section's website.
Once the situation is under control, it says to notify:

- Section contact: chief officer Trond Nilsen, **+47 908 67 560**.
- If he cannot be reached, notify the section's safety officer or section leader.
- The chief officer immediately notifies the secretary general.
- The secretary general notifies the federation's presidency.
- The chief officer sends an SMS to the board and professional-leadership group, and if relevant, the club's professional leader.

Phone lists can change.
Use the current list on the NLF website under documents.
Also see [Safety and emergency procedures](/en/sikkerhet/nodprosedyrer).
