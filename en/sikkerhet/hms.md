# Health, safety and environment

The club must consider the safety of members, volunteers, employees and others at events, work parties, trips and in club facilities.

> **TO RESOLVE:** The board should define how risks are assessed and followed up for events, trips, work parties, facility use and work on club equipment. Assign owners and link to the current internal-control procedure if one exists.

If the club has employees, workplace safety procedures must be clear. See the [managing director's staff procedures](/en/dagleg-leiar/personalrutinar), [emergency procedures](/en/sikkerhet/nodprosedyrer) and [equipment page](/en/klubbutstyr/utstyr).
