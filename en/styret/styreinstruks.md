# Board Instructions for Voss HPK

> **Status: consolidated draft — not confirmed as adopted.** This page combines the two submitted versions. They differ on meeting frequency, reporting, self-evaluation and deputy attendance. The board must resolve the points below before treating this as current policy. Check it against the current bylaws and NIF rules.

## Purpose and scope

These instructions support effective, orderly and responsible board work at Voss Hang- og Paragliderklubb. They take effect when adopted by the board and may be amended or withdrawn by board decision, provided this does not conflict with the bylaws or higher-level rules.

## Board responsibilities

The board shall:

1. Ensure the club is properly organised and that its administration has suitable routines, resources and skills.
2. Exercise overall oversight of the managing director and daily operations.
3. Monitor the club's finances and ensure accounting and use of funds are properly controlled.
4. Consider matters of unusual nature or major importance, such as significant investments, substantial borrowing or major changes to operations.
5. Call the annual general meeting within the deadline in the current bylaws and NIF rules, with the required agenda and documents.
6. Ensure decisions made by the annual general meeting and higher-level sports organisations are implemented.
7. Set goals and a budget for the coming year by 30 November, unless the bylaws or annual cycle set another deadline.
8. Evaluate its own work at least annually.

## Managing director: reporting and cooperation

The managing director reports to the board and keeps the chair informed between meetings about matters requiring attention. The managing director runs daily operations within applicable rules, board decisions, instructions and delegated authority. Matters of unusual nature or major importance require board consideration or specific authority.

The managing director shall:

- provide the board with monthly updates on activity, finances and development;
- present periodic accounts and a liquidity overview, and report on significant contracts, organisation, compliance and required action;
- prepare matters with the chair and ensure decision papers are adequate;
- propose a meeting calendar with the chair after the AGM;
- implement board decisions and communicate them to relevant people;
- notify the board when important assumptions behind earlier decisions change;
- inform the chair of resignations by key personnel and personal absence exceeding three days;
- attend board meetings with speaking rights but no vote, unless excused for a particular matter;
- call an extraordinary board meeting when an important event requires one.

The board shall evaluate the managing director annually.

## Meetings and notice

The board meets at least every other month. The chair calls meetings and decides whether they are in person or online. The agenda and papers should be sent at least seven days in advance, unless exceptional or urgent circumstances require shorter notice. Matters requiring a decision should include a clear proposed resolution.

Deputy members receive the papers. If a board member is absent, the chair invites a deputy as soon as possible.

## Handling matters and decisions

The chair leads meetings. If absent, the board appoints another chair for the meeting. The managing director, or a person appointed by them, presents matters.

The board may decide matters when the quorum requirements in the current bylaws and NIF rules are met. Members and the managing director must not take part in handling or deciding matters in which they are conflicted. Decisions require a majority under the applicable rules. A tie is resolved according to the current bylaws.

At the end of a meeting, the board reserves time to evaluate its work without the managing director or other administration staff present. The chair gives the managing director relevant feedback afterwards.

## Minutes

Minutes shall record at least the time, place or meeting format, participants, matters and decisions. Where relevant, they briefly record the basis for decisions, conflicts of interest and dissent. Minutes should be available to board members within seven days and signed as required by current rules and bylaws. Signed minutes are stored in the club's approved board archive.

## Confidentiality

Board members must prevent sensitive information learned through their role from being shared with unauthorised people. Board discussions are confidential unless the board decides otherwise; decisions may be communicated unless restricted for a specific matter.
