**Source pricing note:** The document says both that a passenger may request video or photos for up to NOK 250, that video cannot be sold separately under VAT rules, and that a flight with video costs NOK 3,250. Clarify this wording and practice before treating it as current pricing.

Tandem instructors must not use prices other than those listed above.

## Transport and payment

- Use the club car when available; otherwise, a private car may be used.
- Submit mileage reimbursement to the club. The source links to [instructor payment information](https://vosshpk.no/kurs/for-instruktorar/).
- Use Vipps or Zettle. The source says each pilot should have a separate account linked to the club so payments can be reconciled.

## Logging and settlement

- Log every flight as required by the contract.
- Register every passenger in the booking system.
- Attach your own list of flights and who received the payment to the reimbursement form.

The source describes payment and reconciliation procedures as work in progress. Confirm the current process with the club before requesting payment.
