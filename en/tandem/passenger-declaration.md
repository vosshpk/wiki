# Tandem Passenger Declaration

The tandem passenger declaration is the form that the tandem pilot and passenger review before a tandem flight.
It records that the passenger has been informed about the risks and insurance coverage and includes fields for flight details, declarations and signatures.
Use one of the official NLF/HPS forms below.

## Official forms from NLF/HPS

Choose the form based on whether the declaration covers one flight or multiple flights with the same named pilot.
NLF/HPS's [instructor page](https://nlf.no/grener/hps/medlemssider-hps/for-instruktorer/){target=_blank} lists the official forms and should be checked before printing.

## Before the flight

- The tandem pilot reviews the form and insurance coverage with the passenger before signing.
- Passengers under 18 must have signatures from both guardians as required by the form.
- Complete and sign the paper form.
- Follow the club's approved procedures for securely storing and deleting completed forms.
- Do not upload signed forms, names, signatures or health information to the wiki or Git repository.
