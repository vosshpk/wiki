# Practical Info Veko

> Summary of the practical guide for Ekstremsportveko.
> Follow current Veko and club information.

## Organisation and bookings

The source planned for two volunteers at the club van from Monday, with daily follow-up by club administration.
Pilots were to handle their own bookings where possible and confirm requests.
Walk-in passengers were encouraged to use the booking system; if a pilot could fly immediately, volunteers could record it on a form.

Pilots were encouraged to show up even without advance bookings, check the weather and recruit passengers at landing and launch.
Bookings were promoted on the Veko and Visit Voss websites, with posters, QR codes and a video.
Transport needs to Aurland or other sites had to be communicated early so a driver could be arranged.
The source encouraged pilots to save the reservation form and pilot panel as phone shortcuts; confirm that the links and system are still in use.
It also listed a summer gondola pass at NOK 1,390.

### Pilot procedures

- Use “working hours” for longer availability periods, otherwise “special days”.
  Enter only times when you can actually fly.
  The gondola was listed as open 10:00–19:30.
- Select only flight types for which you are qualified and prepared.
  For under-18 flights, the source required a police certificate and a declaration form.
  Do not offer Aurland if you cannot travel there.
- The passenger submits a request; the pilot confirms it before it becomes an appointment.
  The pilot panel offered confirmed, pending, cancelled or declined.
- Requests could be submitted without a fixed limit on how far ahead they were made.
- Check the pilot panel and email daily, including spam.
  The source notes that automated email alerts sometimes failed.
- Requests were assigned using a round-robin/priority approach.
  Volunteers could change details or reassign bookings.
  Flights that did not take place had to be cancelled.
- After confirmation, passengers received the meeting point at the club van and the pilot's contact details, plus notice that weather could cause short-notice cancellations.

### Volunteers and passenger forms

The source describes three eight-hour volunteer shifts, especially to help on good-weather days.
A volunteer meeting at 15:00 on Saturday was planned to distribute T-shirts and wristbands; items could otherwise be collected from Sunday with festival access.
Meals were ordered, though the source says the order was too small.
Pilots who had already bought a festival ticket could request reimbursement under the procedure at the time; the source names the managing director as contact and another staff member as processing the refund.

Norwegian and English declaration forms were available at the club van.
During Veko, volunteers were to file them in a binder, photograph them and email them to `tandembooking@vosshpk.no` for digital filing.
For a passenger under 18 with separated parents, the source required both guardians' signatures.
Confirm current requirements and secure storage before handling such forms.

The prices from the source must not be confused with [tandem compensation](/en/tandem/compensation).
The source mentions Zettle and Vipps.
Flights and payments were to be recorded so the club and pilots could reconcile settlement.

### Launch, club tandem equipment and gear

Pilots were to coordinate with the launch marshal.
The left launch at Hangurstoppen was prioritised for tandem and event activity; the marshal could organise the queue and help lay out wings when capacity allowed.

Club tandem equipment was to be reserved using the form linked from the then-current Facebook group.
Check availability before confirming and record the reservation immediately.
The last person using the rig each day was responsible for drying it; it had to be back in the club van the next morning.
Pilots could arrange loans between themselves, and the source encouraged a consistent hire rate.
It also mentions three selfie sticks, two compatible with phones, a GoPro and volunteer help transferring photos/video when capacity allowed.

### Logging, agreement and cooperation

All tandem flights were to be logged in Flightlog each day, including use of the club tandem rig, so flight counts and compensation could be checked.
Tandem pilots flying for the club were to sign the tandem agreement; the source says paper copies were kept in the club van.

The source emphasises cooperation, clear communication, safety and a good working relationship between pilots and volunteers.
