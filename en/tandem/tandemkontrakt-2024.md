# Tandem Agreement 2024 — Archived Source Document

> **Do not use as a current agreement.** This page summarises “Voss HPK Tandem Agreement 2024”. No adoption date is stated, and prices, tax statements, payment methods, booking tools and rules may be outdated. The board and technical lead must confirm the current agreement and check legal and tax matters with NLF and the relevant authorities. Signed agreements and passenger information must not be stored in the wiki or Git.

## Purpose and scope

The source says the agreement governs the relationship between Voss HPK and the tandem instructor. According to the source, paid introductory flights from sites naturally belonging to Voss HPK must be flown by full or associate members, and the instructor must sign the agreement. The source describes tandem flying in Norway as non-commercial and says flights are introductory flights, not private marketing by individual pilots. This is a transcription of the 2024 text, not confirmation of current rules.

## Flights, logging and bookings

- Tandem flights organised by the club must go through the club and be logged.
- For passengers under 18, the source says the instructor must be registered in KlubbAdmin with a valid police certificate.
- The source distinguishes flights assigned through the booking system, direct requests flown the same day, unpaid flights with friends/family/sponsors or for training, and flights with pilots in training. All should be logged in Flightlog; paid or directly booked flights must also be entered in the booking system.
- Paid flights booked in advance must be entered in the booking system. If several passengers book directly, flights should be shared fairly among available pilots. Reassignments due to unforeseen absence should also be fair.
- Pilots should only mark themselves available when they can actually fly. The source prohibits manipulating availability to generate more flights.
- The pilot must contact the passenger promptly after assignment, arrange for someone else to do so, or reassign the flight.
- During Ekstremsportveko, use the booking system agreed in advance; pilots are responsible for logging every flight. The source says Veko uses separate prices.
- The source says a liability declaration must be completed and retained by the instructor under the rules at that time.

## Prices and split stated in the 2024 agreement

| Flight | Source price | Source split |
|---|---:|---|
| Standard tandem in Voss | NOK 2,900 | NOK 1,400 to the club; remainder to instructor |
| Aurland | Up to NOK 4,000 | No other split is stated |
| Tow using the club boat | NOK 3,000 | NOK 1,000 each to pilot, boat operator and club |
| Video/photos | Up to NOK 200 | Instructor may set a price up to the stated limit |

The source says discounts may be given to acquaintances or for short flights, but instructors should not use other prices than those listed. The club's share was to cover passenger gondola tickets, driver compensation, administration, booking-system maintenance and equipment investments. Changes to the split were to be announced by email. The source says reselling flights for commission is prohibited. Confirm these points against current decisions and rules before use.

## Payments and settlement

- The source mentions Vipps and Zettle, with a user linked to the club account for each pilot. Payments were to use the current systems; direct bank transfer was also mentioned.
- The pilot was responsible for ensuring that the passenger paid. Cash was an exception; it was to be deposited into the club account promptly, labelled with the pilot's name and “tandem”. An email stating who paid, what it was for and the date was to be sent to `faktura@vosshpk.no`.
- Pilots were to keep travel claims/logs and mileage for tandem-related driving.
- The source states an annual tax-free compensation limit of up to NOK 20,000: up to NOK 10,000 without reporting and up to NOK 10,000 in documented travel/mileage compensation. Claims were to be sent before month-end for payment around the 15th. This is a historical tax claim; check with the Norwegian Tax Administration and the club's accountant before use.
- The source says the club may withhold amounts owed and that accrued funds may be used for tandem-related equipment.

## Safety, conduct and responses

The source requires tandem flying to follow HPS/NLF rules and be conducted safely and professionally. It says the safety group and technical lead handle rule breaches, inform the managing director and board, and report to the HPS chief of section. Any licence suspension is assigned to that chief in the source. Confirm processes and sanctions against current NLF/HPS rules and the club's bylaws.

## Signing

The source document has fields for the tandem instructor's name in block letters, place and date. Use an updated, approved agreement for signatures; do not sign or enter personal information on this archive page.
