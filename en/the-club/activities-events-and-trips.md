# Activities, events and trips

Voss HPK runs flying activities, courses, social gatherings and events.
See the [club website](https://vosshpk.no/) for announced activities.

## Planning

> **TO RESOLVE:** Publish a current activity calendar with a responsible contact, registration details, deadlines and any fees.

For each event or club trip, information should cover:

- the responsible organiser or trip leader
- registration, participant terms and contact details
- transport, accommodation and cost sharing
- safety briefing, weather decision and contingency plan
- procedures for minors, where relevant
- whether a named trip leader has overall responsibility from departure to return, and how participants and guardians receive the instructions

For major recurring events, the club should maintain a dedicated event guide or checklist and identify its owner.
If the club offers para-sport or adapted activities, add information about accessibility, required expertise and where to seek advice or support.

See also [course frameworks](/en/training/course-delivery-framework) and [emergency procedures](/en/safety/safety-and-emergency-procedures).
