# Membership

Membership of Voss HPK connects you with the club and may provide access to shared facilities and transport arrangements.
See the separate page about [member benefits](/en/the-club/member-benefits).

## Join the club

- **Annual membership fee:** Check the [club’s current membership page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/bli-medlem/) before joining.
- **Join online:** [Register through MinIdrett](https://medlemskap.nif.no/Start/Index/25357).
- **Membership card:** Find the digital card in your MinIdrett profile or app.
  Some partner discounts require it.
- **Licence and insurance:** Independent flying also requires a valid licence and insurance through the [Norwegian Air Sports Federation (NLF)](https://nlf.no).

## Membership and activity fees

Check the current membership fee on the [club's membership page](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/bli-medlem/).
Course, training and participant fees may vary by activity.

## Membership and flying safety

Members using club sites and shared facilities must follow current NLF/HPAC safety rules, [local Voss flying rules](/en/safety/local-flying-safety-rules), and [emergency procedures](/en/safety/safety-and-emergency-procedures).

## Update or end membership

Update your contact details, address or membership through your personal [MinIdrett](https://minidrett.nif.no) profile.
