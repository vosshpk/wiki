# Volunteering and roles

The club relies on volunteers.
Volunteering must remain voluntary; tasks and expectations should be clear and assigned predictably.

> **TO RESOLVE:** Maintain an up-to-date list of volunteer tasks, owners, contacts and any reimbursement or expense coverage.
> Confirm which roles require election, appointment or specific training, and what agreement or membership is required for people taking on elected or ongoing responsibilities.

For air-sports training, see [courses and pilot progression](/en/training/training-and-pilot-progression), the [board and committees](/en/board/board), and the [safety group](/en/safety/safety-group).
Club events and trips are covered under [activities](/en/the-club/activities-events-and-trips).
