# Course materials

This page summarises course documents and learning resources. See [course materials on vosshpk.no](https://vosshpk.no/kursmateriale/) for the latest files and forms.

**Always use the current NLF/HPS safety system and training programme.** Older files or practice questions may be out of date. Current programme requirements and instructor guidance take precedence over this overview.

## Documents and forms

The course materials page includes, among other things:

- the safety system and training programmes for paragliding (PG), speedgliding (SPG), hang gliding (HG) and powered paragliding (PPG)
- the accident response plan
- course agreements for PG and SPG students
- declarations required for a particular course, including trial-day and tandem forms

Submit any agreements or forms that must be signed before the course by the deadline given by your instructor. Use the current versions from the course materials page.

## Teaching and practice resources

- HPS beginner-course teaching materials are stored in the club's internal Google Drive. Contact the course coordinator for access.
- Progression cards give an overview of levels and courses.
- The PP2–PP5 question set can be used for revision, but the club notes that some content may be out of date. Check answers against current learning material.
- Kahoot quizzes cover aerodynamics, meteorology, practical flying, equipment knowledge, first aid, and laws and regulations.

## Course evaluation

Students should complete the course evaluation no later than seven days after the course ends. Use the form linked from [the course materials page](https://vosshpk.no/kursmateriale/), which provides Norwegian and English versions.

For practice and progression guidance, see [Progression](/en/training-information/progression) and [Pilot Progression](/en/training-information/pilot-progression). Instructor frameworks are in [Instructor Information](/en/instructor-information/course-framework).
