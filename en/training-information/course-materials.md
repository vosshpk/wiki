# Course materials

This page brings together the documents, forms and practice resources listed by Voss HPK on its [course materials page](https://vosshpk.no/kursmateriale/). Use current editions of the safety system and training programmes, and follow your instructor's directions.

## Course evaluation

Students must complete the evaluation within seven days of the course ending. The Voss HPK page lists Norwegian and English SurveyMonkey forms, but both older links returned 404 when checked. The page links a [new evaluation form](https://forms.gle/ZFB5aLPd8DZ7uEk28) as their replacement.

See [Progression](/en/training-information/progression), [Pilot Progression](/en/training-information/pilot-progression) and the [course framework for instructors](/en/instructor-information/course-framework).
