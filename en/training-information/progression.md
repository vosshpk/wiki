# Progression

Progress comes from regular, safe practice and useful feedback. [Voss HPK's progression page](https://vosshpk.no/progresjon-paragliding-speedriding/) gives practice ideas for wing control, speedgliding and PP2 progression.

**Always follow current privileges, the NLF/HPS programme and your instructor's limits.** Only practise skills you have been taught and approved to practise, at a suitable site and in safe conditions.

## Practice and ground handling

Ground handling and kiting build wing control at every level. They are especially useful with new equipment or after a break from flying. Practise with another pilot when possible.

Choose an open area and conditions you can manage. Wear a helmet, keep control of the lines, check for obstacles and plan what you will do if the wind increases. Think about how much room you have to stop and bring the wing down.

Wing control includes keeping the wing balanced and adjusting line pressure with the brakes and body position. Start with one input at a time. Progress gradually through smooth inflations, controlled forward and reverse launches, transitions between launch positions and bringing the wing down calmly. Maintain contact with the brakes and return to neutral contact after a correction.

Voss HPK's exercise list also includes more demanding drills. Exercises that cause sudden wing movement or simulate collapses, turbulence or spins should only be considered when an instructor confirms they are permitted and suitable for your level and conditions. Do not progress to harder exercises without guidance.

## SPG2 foot-launch course

Voss HPK's page says a pilot needs a valid SPG2 rating and flights from at least five different sites before taking a foot-launch course. After the course, the pilot should be able to perform forward, reverse and aborted launches, flare and land safely, and understand the hazards of foot-launching a speedglider.

The page notes an exception for SPG2 student ratings issued before 29 November 2021. Confirm any exception and current requirements with an instructor and the latest training programme before planning a foot launch.

## From PP2 towards PP3

Voss HPK's guide summarises PP2 privileges as:

- ground handling on flat ground
- glide flights from sites where the student has been checked out
- practice in simple lift only after a briefing and approval from an instructor for that specific flight

Review new techniques and the requirements for the next level with an instructor before trying them. Key topics include assessing conditions, making safe decisions, launching, approach patterns and landing. Exercises involving turns, collapses or speedbar must stay within the scope your instructor has approved for your level.

Plan the approach early and with height margin. Avoid crosswind, tailwind, strong or unstable wind and turbulent conditions unless you are approved to fly in them. Do not fly in lift without prior briefing and approval. If you enter lift without approval, follow your instructor's guidance and move to calmer air or land at the planned site.

For courses and a local overview of ratings, see [Pilot Progression](/en/training-information/pilot-progression) and the [course calendar](https://vosshpk.no/kurs/).
