## Applying for a student rating

After completing the beginner course and passing the theory exam, you have met the training programme requirements and can apply for a student rating through the e-course. Follow [NLF/HPS guidance for a new rating](https://nlf.no/grener/hps/sikkerhet--utdanning/kompetanser/nytt-niva/), select the correct level—SPG2 or PP2—complete the application and notify your chosen instructor.

## Renewing a rating

The renewal period for the next year opens on 1 November each year. Student ratings SPG2/PP2 require instructor approval for renewal. Follow [NLF/HPS renewal and reinstatement guidance](https://nlf.no/grener/hps/sikkerhet--utdanning/kompetanser/fornyelse--gjenopptagelse/) and use its e-course for instructions.

## Follow-up

If you did not complete the progression required for your rating during the beginner course, the club may sometimes be able to include extra students in a later beginner course for follow-up. Students on that course take priority for equipment and instructor capacity. Make a follow-up plan with your course instructor or email the managing director. The club schedules extra follow-up when needed; keep up to date by joining the [student group on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/elevar.i.voss.hpk/).

### Catch-up weekend

Voss HPK holds a free catch-up long weekend near the end of the season for students from current and previous beginner courses in SPG and PG. The club encourages everyone to reserve the weekend, including pilots who already have their rating: it offers a safe place to keep learning and meet others at a similar stage. The club also tries to arrange social activities.

The SPG weekend is held in Myrkdalen or from Hangurstoppen, depending on the weather. PG pilots can use the Voss launch sites that are available, and begin at the level they last flew. Sign-up details and other activities during the year are posted in the [club calendar](https://vosshpk.no/om-klubben/aarshjul/).

## Equipment

Your main instructor from the beginner course must approve your wing and harness. Agree in writing on which equipment you will use.

### SPG2

The club lists a minimum wing area of 11.8 m² and a maximum wing loading of 7 kg/m². Pilots in all weight classes may use a 14.5 m² wing, even when that puts the wing loading above 7 kg/m².

Parakites are not approved at student or SPG2 level. This includes moustache and line wings and similar designs, even if the wing area otherwise matches the pilot's weight. The club explains that these wings require experience and good wing control.

### PP2

Equipment must be suitable for student-level flying. An instructor may refuse equipment that is unsuitable for your level.

## Site check-outs

An instructor must give a site check-out, including an in-person briefing at launch and landing. Review the flight plan, hazards and obstacles, local conditions and rules, and any alternate landing areas. If someone other than an instructor is to give the check-out, an instructor must review and approve this in advance. A check-out applies only to the conditions present when it was given.

### SPG2

At a new site, the check-out must be given by an instructor or a pilot whom your instructor considers qualified. Voss HPK's student guide sets the wind limit at 7 m/s, measured as the strongest gust during a ten-minute period.

### PP2

PP2 students may fly only at a training hill or student launch, and only in calm, stable conditions. A new-site check-out must be given by an instructor or a pilot whom your instructor considers qualified. The wind limit is 5 m/s, measured as the strongest gust during a ten-minute period.

The club advises students against flying abroad without a Para Pro instructor or a pilot authorised by their personal instructor. Confirm current limits and requirements before flying.
