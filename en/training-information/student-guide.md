# Student Guide

This guide summarises practical information for students after a beginner course. [Voss HPK's current student guide](https://vosshpk.no/etter-fullfort-kurs/) links to forms, the course calendar and contact details.

**Follow the current NLF/HPS training programme and safety system.** Privileges, equipment requirements and wind limits can change. Your instructor's assessment and the latest rules take precedence over this summary.

## After the course and student rating

After completing the beginner course and passing the theory exam, follow NLF's instructions to apply for the correct rating through its e-course. The club guide points students to SPG2 for speedgliding and PP2 for paragliding. Select the correct level, complete the application and notify your instructor.

The club guide says annual renewal of student ratings normally opens on 1 November and requires instructor approval. Confirm the date and process in NLF's e-course before renewing.

Complete the course evaluation no later than seven days after the course ends. Norwegian and English forms are linked from [Course Materials](/en/training-information/course-materials).

## Questions and follow-up

For privileges, requirements and progression, first check the current NLF/HPS training programme and safety system. Take course questions to your main instructor or the technical director. Questions about Min Idrett invoices and insurance can go to NLF member services at [post@nlf.no](mailto:post@nlf.no). Use the club's [current contact details](/en/the-club/about) to reach the managing director.

If you did not complete the planned progression during the beginner course, make a follow-up plan with your instructor. Extra coaching or a place on a later course depends on instructor and equipment capacity. Follow the course calendar and club channels for catch-up weekends and other activities. Voss HPK's guide describes a free annual catch-up weekend for PG and SPG; confirm the date and terms in the current announcement.

## Equipment and site check-outs

Agree in writing with your main instructor which wing and harness you may use. The instructor must approve equipment for your level.

Voss HPK's student guide lists these local SPG2 limits: minimum wing area 11.8 m² and maximum wing loading 7 kg/m². It also says that 14.5 m² may be used by all weight classes even if the loading then exceeds 7 kg/m², and that parakites are not approved at student or SPG2 level. For PP2, equipment must be suitable for student flying; an instructor may refuse unsuitable equipment. **Always confirm these figures and equipment requirements against current NLF rules and with your instructor before flying.**

A site check-out should include a briefing at launch and landing, flight plan, hazards, local rules and any alternate landing areas. A check-out is valid only for the conditions in which it was given. If someone other than an instructor is to give it, the instructor must approve this in advance.

The club guide lists these local wind limits, measured as the strongest gust in a ten-minute period:

- SPG2 student: no more than 7 m/s.
- PP2 student: no more than 5 m/s, and only at a training hill or student launch in calm, stable conditions.

For a new site, an SPG2 student needs a check-out from an instructor or a pilot the instructor considers qualified. A PP2 student needs a check-out from an instructor or a pilot approved by the instructor. The guide advises students not to fly abroad without a Para Pro instructor or a pilot authorised by their personal instructor. Always confirm current rules before flying.

## Important numbers

The club guide lists these emergency numbers: medical emergency 113, police 112, out-of-hours medical service 116 117 and non-emergency police 02800. It also lists ski patrol contacts for Myrkdalen (+47 415 18 002) and Voss Resort (+47 455 11 283). Check local patrol numbers before your trip.

See the club's [emergency procedures](/en/safety/emergency-procedures), [Progression guidance](/en/training-information/progression) and [course calendar](https://vosshpk.no/kurs/).
