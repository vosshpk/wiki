# Pilot Progression

Voss HPK offers beginner and further training, tandem check-outs and instructor training in cooperation with the Norwegian Air Sports Federation (NLF/HPAC).
Training and licensing must follow current NLF programmes and safety requirements.
Course dates, prices, registration and admission requirements may change.
Check the [club’s current course pages](https://vosshpk.no/kurs/){target=_blank} and [post-course student guide](https://vosshpk.no/etter-fullfort-kurs/){target=_blank} before registering or planning further progression.

## Training offered

1. **Paragliding beginner course (spring, summer and autumn):** Theory at the [clubhouse at Bømoen](/en/club-equipment/clubhouse), ground handling and low training hops at the [training hill](/en/flying/sites), followed by radio-guided high flights from [Hangurstoppen](/en/flying/sites) to Prestegardslandet.
2. **Speed-riding beginner course (winter):** Paraglider wing and skis in Myrkdalen and Bavallen, with theory at the clubhouse.
3. **Tandem training:** For experienced pilots seeking approval to fly with a passenger.
4. **Instructor training:** For pilots with a PP4 or PP5 rating who want to support recruitment and training.
   Contact the safety group at [sikkerheit@vosshpk.no](mailto:sikkerheit@vosshpk.no).

## NLF/HPAC progression overview

This is a brief local guide, not a substitute for current NLF requirements or an instructor’s assessment.

| Stage / rating | Brief description | Local context |
|---|---|---|
| Student / course | Training with an instructor | Theory at Bømoen, ground handling at Bakkatun, high flights from Hangurstoppen East |
| PP2 | Student pilot certificate with restrictions | Hanguren East and suitable launches in calm, non-thermic conditions |
| PP3 | Independent pilot | Most club launches when wind and weather are suitable |
| PP4 | Advanced pilot | Thermic and cross-country flying, more demanding launches and winter top-landings |
| PP5 | Senior pilot | May support training, lead trips and pursue instructor training |
| SPG1–SPG3 | Speedglider / speed-riding check-outs | Ski and speed flying in Bavallen and Myrkdalen |

## After the beginner course

- Join club evenings at the [club trailer in Vangen](/en/club-equipment/trailer) and fly with experienced pilots.
- Practise wing control and launch technique at the [training hill](/en/flying/sites) when it is open.
- Ask an experienced pilot or instructor about launch conditions, wind and airspace before setting up if uncertain.
